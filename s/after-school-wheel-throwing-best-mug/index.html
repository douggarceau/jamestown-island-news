After-School Wheel Throwing for Grades 3 to 6

A Nov. 16–25 Arts After School session teaches the basics of wheel throwing, and a mixed-media series follows.

The Arts Center’s Arts After School program is an enrichment program for students in grades 3 through 6. Classes meet on school days from 3:15 to 5:00 p.m., and an after-school snack is included. Families must pre-register at JamestownArts.com.

The session called Best Mug, led by Samantha Lokemoen, runs Nov. 16 through Nov. 25 and costs $96. Students learn the basics of wheel throwing.

Next session

Beginner Crafting with Siggy follows, running Nov. 30 through Dec. 11 for $120. The series is described as a set of mixed media projects.

The center’s fall newsletter lists both sessions along with its other youth and adult offerings for September through December.
