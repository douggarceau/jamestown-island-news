Culinary Classes at the Arts Center Through December

Pad Thai, homemade ramen, soup and bread, and a takeout-style class are on the Arts Center's December schedule, each starting at 4 p.m.

Jamestown Arts Center's culinary classes continue through December, with four sessions in the newsletter that each start at 4:00 p.m.

On Wednesday, Dec. 2, the class is Pad Thai, for $65, or $52 for members. On Saturday, Dec. 5, students make Homemade Ramen for $60, or $48 for members.

The series returns to soup on Monday, Dec. 21, with Soup & Simple Bread at $45, or $36 for members. The last class of the month is Better Than Takeout on Dec. 28, for $65, or $52 for members.

The member price is the second figure listed for each class in the newsletter. The newsletter does not give a sign-up method for these classes, so check JamestownArts.com before planning a visit.

The culinary classes are a steady part of the Arts Center's winter schedule, and they sit alongside its children's art classes and its Second Saturday workshops. Readers who want the full list of classes and dates can find them in the center's Sept.–Dec. newsletter.
