Winter Watercolor and Martian Plants for Kids in December

Two Saturday art sessions for children are on the Arts Center's December schedule, one for ages 3 to 6 and one for grades 1 to 5.

Two Saturday art sessions for children are on the Jamestown Arts Center's December schedule. Both are listed with online sign-up at JamestownArts.com.

On Saturday, Dec. 5, Step One: Winter Watercolor is for ages 3 to 6. Sessions are at 10 a.m. or 1 p.m. The cost is $10 per child, or $8 for members, and registration is online.

On Saturday, Dec. 12, Second Saturdays: Martian Plant Life is for grades 1 to 5. Sessions are at 10:30 a.m. or 1 p.m. The cost is $15 per child, or $12 for members. The newsletter says families should sign up at least 24 hours ahead, and seats are limited.

Arts After School

The newsletter also lists Arts After School, a crafting program for grades 3 to 6 that meets on school days from 3:15 to 5 p.m. with an after-school snack. Its Beginner Crafting with Siggy session runs Nov. 30 through Dec. 11 and costs $120. Pre-registration is required online.

Times come from each organizer's listing. Check before you go.
