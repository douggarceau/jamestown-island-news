Martian Plant Life for Grades 1 to 5 on Dec. 12

The Arts Center's Second Saturday class for grades 1 to 5 runs in two sessions on Dec. 12, with sign-up at least 24 hours in advance.

The Second Saturday children's program at Jamestown Arts Center is Martian Plant Life, for students in grades 1 to 5, on Saturday, Dec. 12. Students can choose a morning session from 10:30 a.m. to noon or an afternoon session from 1:00 to 2:30 p.m.

The class costs $15 per child, or $12 per child for Arts Center members.

Registration is required. Families sign up online at JamestownArts.com, and the newsletter asks that sign-up happen at least 24 hours before the class. Seats are limited, so families who want a specific time should register early.

The class is one of several December programs at the center. The Step One watercolor class for ages 3 to 6 meets on Dec. 5, and the culinary classes run through the end of the month, as listed in the newsletter.

The newsletter lists both session times and the sign-up requirement, so families can plan ahead.
