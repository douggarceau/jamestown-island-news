Decorate an Ornament at the Arts Center on Monday, Dec. 7

A drop-in glaze night from 6 to 9 p.m. on Dec. 7, with ornaments fired over the weekend and ready to collect the following Monday.

Jamestown Arts Center is hosting a drop-in ornament decorating session on Monday, Dec. 7. The session starts at 6:00 p.m. and runs until 9:00 p.m. The newsletter lists no advance registration for it.

Guests decorate pre-made ornaments, which are glazed on the spot. The pieces are fired over the weekend, and they can be picked up the following Monday, so families should plan a second trip to the Arts Center to take them home.

The cost is $15 per ornament. The session is open to all ages. Children under 12 need a parent or guardian to supervise them.

The evening suits families wrapping up a busy holiday week, and anyone who wants a simple handmade gift. The center's December calendar also includes a children's watercolor class on Dec. 5, a plant workshop for grades 1 to 5 on Dec. 12 and a run of culinary classes, all listed in its newsletter.

For more information, visit JamestownArts.com, the center's website.
