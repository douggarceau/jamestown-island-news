Make an Ornament, Then Cook a Side: Arts Center Classes

A drop-in ornament night on Dec. 7 and a holiday sides cooking class on Dec. 14 and 19 fill the Arts Center calendar.

The Jamestown Arts Center has two hands-on classes on its December calendar, one for crafting and one for cooking.

Ceramic Ornament Decorating is a drop-in session on Monday, Dec. 7, starting at 6 p.m. at The Arts Center. The admission is $15. The newsletter lists the session as drop-in and does not state a registration requirement.

Holiday Sides

Holiday Sides is a cooking class offered on two dates, Monday, Dec. 14 and Saturday, Dec. 19. Each date has sessions at 3 p.m. and 4 p.m. The cost is $60, or $48 for Arts Center members. The newsletter does not state a registration requirement for the cooking classes, so anyone interested should contact The Arts Center before the session.

The same calendar lists a Soup & Simple Bread class at 4 p.m. on Monday, Dec. 21, for $45, or $36 for members.

Times come from each organizer's listing. Check before you go.
