Arts Center Sets Winter Lineup, Starting With Nov. 20 Show

Molly Kugler Dickinson’s “Of Longing and Loss” runs Nov. 20 through Jan. 9, followed by a ceramics show in late winter.

The Jamestown Arts Center has set its winter exhibition schedule, and the first show is a new exhibition by Molly Kugler Dickinson. “Of Longing and Loss” runs from Nov. 20, 2026, through Jan. 9, 2027, according to the center’s upcoming exhibitions page. Dickinson was also named the 2025 Best-in-Show winner in the center’s members’ exhibition, the page notes.

The gallery is open Wednesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and by appointment, according to the center’s website. The Arts Center is at 18 Valley Street. The exhibition page does not list an opening reception, admission or a registration requirement, so check the center’s site before planning a visit.

What comes next

The next scheduled show is “Janus: Facing Past and Future Together,” running Jan. 30 through March 27, 2027. It is curated by Meagan Hepp and Lyric Marie Johnson and features Ceramic Artists in Research and Practice, known as C•A•R•P.

Also on view this month, Viktoria Lubchenko’s “Portraits of Unspoken Truth” runs Nov. 12 through Dec. 12, according to the center’s fall newsletter. The newsletter lists the show’s dates but no reception time.
