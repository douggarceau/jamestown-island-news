Winter Watercolor for Ages 3 to 6 on Saturday, Dec. 5

The Arts Center's Step One class offers two one-hour watercolor sessions on Dec. 5, one from 10 to 11 a.m. and one from 1 to 2 p.m.

Jamestown Arts Center's Step One class for young artists offers Winter Watercolor on Saturday, Dec. 5, for children ages 3 to 6. Each child can attend one of two one-hour sessions, one from 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. and one from 1:00 to 2:00 p.m.

The cost is $10 per child, or $8 per child for Arts Center members.

Registration is online only. Families sign up at JamestownArts.com, as the newsletter directs.

Step One is one of several family-friendly programs the center runs this month. Its children's class for grades 1 to 5, Martian Plant Life, meets on Saturday, Dec. 12, and its culinary classes run through the end of December. The newsletter lists all of these with prices and dates, so families can plan a full month of visits.

The newsletter offers two sessions on the same Saturday, one in the morning and one in the early afternoon, so families can choose the time that fits their weekend.
