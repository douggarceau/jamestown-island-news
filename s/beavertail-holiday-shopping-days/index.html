Beavertail Light Reopens Its Shop for the Holidays

The lighthouse museum and gift shop open for four December afternoons, Dec. 5, 6, 12 and 13, from noon to 3 p.m.

The Beavertail Lighthouse Museum and gift shop closed for the season in the fall, but the volunteers who run it are opening the doors again for four December afternoons.

According to the Beavertail Lighthouse Museum Association, the museum and shop open for holiday shopping on Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 5 and 6, and again on Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 12 and 13, from noon to 3 p.m. each day. The museum sits inside Beavertail State Park at the southern tip of the island.

What's in the shop

The gift shop carries lighthouse mugs, ornaments and books, including histories of Beavertail itself, the kind of gift that means something to anyone who grew up looking at that light.

Santa at the lighthouse

The December open house has its own traditions. In past years the Jamestown Fire Department has helped hang a lighted wreath on the granite tower, with Santa riding up on the ladder truck, weather permitting. Inside, board member Bob Langton has played Santa, greeting visitors and reading a holiday story to the children while volunteers set out refreshments. The association hasn't announced this year's open-house plans yet, so check beavertaillight.org before you go.

A little history

A light has stood at Beavertail since 1749, one of the oldest light stations on the Atlantic coast, and it still guides ships into Narragansett Bay. The museum is in the keeper's house, built in 1856, and the assistant keeper's house next door dates to 1898. Volunteers have run the museum for about three decades.

A plate with a purpose

For a gift that bolts onto a car, the association also promotes its Rhode Island charity license plate. The plate costs $43.50 for a front-and-back set, with $20 of each order going to the association and $23.50 covering production, per the association's site. Orders go by mail to the RI DMV plate office in Cranston, with the printed order form, payment by check or credit card, and a copy of your current registration.

Plan your visit

Beavertail is about a 10-minute drive south from the village along Beavertail Road. The park's ocean views are at their most dramatic in winter, but the point is exposed and windy, so dress warmly. The association's 2027 calendar will be posted on its website when it is ready, and it is recruiting volunteers. The museum can be reached at 401-423-3270.
