Beavertail Light Reopens Its Shop for the Holidays

The lighthouse museum, closed for the season, will open its gift shop on two December weekends.

The Beavertail Lighthouse Museum and gift shop has closed for the season, but the association that runs it is opening the doors again for four December afternoons.

According to the Beavertail Lighthouse Museum Association, the museum and shop will open for holiday shopping on Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 5 and 6, and again on Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 12 and 13, from noon to 3 p.m. each day. The museum sits inside Beavertail State Park.

A plate with a purpose

For a gift that bolts onto a car, the association also promotes its Rhode Island charity license plate. The plate costs $43.50 for a front-and-back set, with $20 of each order going to the association and $23.50 covering production, per the association's site. Orders go by mail to the RI DMV plate office in Cranston, with the printed order form, payment by check or credit card, and a copy of your current registration.

> A $43.50 plate, with $20 going to the lighthouse.

A stormy finish

The season ended on a rough note. The association says the museum and gift shop were closed on Saturday and Sunday, Sept. 26 and 27, because of severe weather, and the tower climb planned for Sept. 26 was cancelled.

The association says its 2027 calendar will be posted on its website as soon as it is available, and it is recruiting volunteers to help at the lighthouse. Details on both are at beavertaillight.org, and the museum can be reached at (401) 423-3270.
