Grades 3 to 6 Can Make a Mug in Arts After School

Best Mug runs on school days from Nov. 16 to 25 at the Arts Center, and spots are booked online.

Best Mug is an Arts After School class at the Jamestown Arts Center for students in grades 3 through 6. It meets on school days from Nov. 16 through Nov. 25, from 3:15 to 5 p.m. The fee is $96.

Booking

Pre-registration is required and is handled online at JamestownArts.com. The Arts Center's after-school sessions begin when the school day ends, which makes them an easy add-on for families on the island.

A second session, Beginner Crafting with Siggy, follows from Nov. 30 through Dec. 11, at 3:15 to 5 p.m. on school days, for $120.
