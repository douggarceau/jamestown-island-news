Photographers Gather at the Arts Center on Nov. 17

The Buffalo City Photo Club meets Tuesday evening in the Hansen Studio, and the Arts Center says the session is open to anyone.

Island photographers have an evening date this month. The Buffalo City Photo Club meets at the Jamestown Arts Center on Tuesday, Nov. 17, at 6:30 p.m. in the Hansen Studio.

The newsletter lists the session at no charge and describes it as open to anyone, so people who are not Arts Center members can take part. The listing gives no program topic, so contacting the club or the Arts Center ahead of time is the best way to learn what the evening covers.
