Mrs. Claus and Dickens Carolers Sail From East Ferry

Coastal Queen Cruises runs a Candy Cane Express with Mrs. Claus on Sunday, Nov. 29, and a carolers cruise on Saturday, Dec. 5, both from 1 East Ferry Wharf.

Two holiday cruises leave right from the Jamestown waterfront in the next two weeks, both aboard the Coastal Queen from 1 East Ferry Wharf.

Candy Cane Express with Mrs. Claus, Sunday, Nov. 29

The Candy Cane Express is built for young children. Coastal Queen Cruises lists two sailings on Sunday, Nov. 29, at 11 a.m. and 1 p.m., each about an hour long. The boat cruises Newport Harbor and the bay while Mrs. Claus reads to the children and hot chocolate is served, according to the listing. The cabin is enclosed and heated, and every child gets a candy cane on the way off the boat.

Listed prices are $42 for adults, $30 for children, $26 for young children, and $125 for a family pack of two adults and two children under 12.

Holiday Cruise with the Very Merry Dickens Carolers, Saturday, Dec. 5

On Holiday Stroll Saturday, the Coastal Queen leaves East Ferry at 1:30 p.m. for a 75-minute cruise with the Very Merry Dickens Carolers aboard. It returns at about 2:45 p.m., with time to spare before Santa's arrival and the evening tree lighting. Listed prices are $45 for adults, $40 for seniors and military, and $35 for children and youth.

How to book

Call Coastal Queen Cruises at (401) 423-9900 or email info@cruiseri.com. The listings don't say whether the sailings sell out, so book ahead to be safe.
