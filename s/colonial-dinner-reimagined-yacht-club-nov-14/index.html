Final Colonial Dinner Reimagined at the Yacht Club, Nov. 14

The Jamestown Historical Society's last reimagined Colonial Dinner runs Saturday, Nov. 14, with a buffet, cash bar and clam chowder.

The Jamestown Historical Society is holding the final dinner in its reimagined Colonial Dinner series on Saturday, Nov. 14, at the Conanicut Yacht Club, 40 Bay View Drive. Drinks and appetizers start at 5:30 p.m., and dinner is served at 6 p.m. The listed event window runs until 8 p.m. Parking is in the club's lot across from the building.

Members of the society pay $50, and non-members pay $60. The evening is a cash bar, appetizers and a buffet dinner.

What's on the buffet

The savory courses are built on New England cooking. Guests can start with Rhode Island clam chowder or a savory squash soup, then move on to shepherd's pie, chicken pot pie and corn bread. A salad of spinach, apple, cranberry, goat cheese and pecans rounds out the main table. Dessert is a set of homestyle favorites: pumpkin rum cake, apple cake and vanilla ice cream, with coffee or tea.

Getting a seat

Guests reserve online, and the society asks for RSVPs by Nov. 9. The booking page shows both ticket types as sold out, even though it also lists unlimited availability. Because those two signals conflict, check with the society before you plan around the dinner.

The listing on the society's own events page is the place to confirm times, prices and seating before the weekend. The page does not name a speaker or presenter for the evening.
