Jamestown Community Chorus Sings 'A Contemporary Christmas'

Two December concerts at the Recreation Center, Dec. 12 at 4 p.m. and Dec. 13 at 3 p.m., with mostly 21st-century music.

The Jamestown Community Chorus brings its holiday concert home to the island this December. "A Contemporary Christmas" is on the chorus calendar for Saturday, Dec. 12, at 4 p.m. and Sunday, Dec. 13, at 3 p.m., at the Jamestown Recreation Center, 41 Conanicus Avenue.

The music

Rehearsals began in September. In an October update, the chorus said this year's program is mostly 21st-century music with a few folk tunes, a change of pace from the carol-heavy programs of many holiday concerts. The group last used the "A Contemporary Christmas" title back in 2004.

> Mostly 21st-century music, with a few folk tunes.

A long island tradition

The December concert is one of the island's oldest holiday customs. The chorus was founded in 1949 and gives two concerts a year, in December and May, usually singing four-part music. Past holiday programs have included "An American Christmas" last year, "Silver Bells" and "Two Englands: Christmas Carols from Old England and New England." Some selections in past concerts have had percussion and other instruments alongside the voices.

B.J. Whitehouse directs the chorus. Members rehearse Monday nights from 7 to 9 at Central Baptist Church, 99 Narragansett Avenue, and don't need to read music, because rehearsal recordings are provided. Anyone thinking of joining for the spring season can ask about it at the concert.

Tickets

Ticket details for December hadn't been posted as of this week. For the spring concert, the chorus sold general admission tickets online and at the door. Watch jamestownchorus.com for December tickets.

Make a weekend of it

The Saturday concert falls on the second weekend of Beavertail Lighthouse's holiday shop hours, noon to 3 p.m. on Dec. 12 and 13, so a drive out to the lighthouse and an afternoon concert fit together nicely.
