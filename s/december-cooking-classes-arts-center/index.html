Holiday Cooking Classes at the Arts Center in December

Pad Thai, holiday appetizers and sides, and a soup-and-bread class fill the December culinary calendar.

Beyond the Thanksgiving and Friendsgiving classes, the Arts Center’s culinary program continues into December. Pad Thai is on Dec. 2 at 4:00 p.m., at $65 for non-members and $52 for members. The class is described as exploring the balance of sweet, salty, tangy and savory flavors.

Merry Little Appetizers meets Dec. 7 and Dec. 12, with sessions at 3:00 and 4:00 p.m. each day. Holiday Sides follows on Dec. 14 and Dec. 19 at the same times. Each costs $60 for non-members and $48 for members.

Soup and bread

Soup and Simple Bread is offered Dec. 21 at 4:00 p.m. for $45, or $36 for members, after an earlier session on Nov. 23. The newsletter does not list a registration requirement or a location for these classes, so check the Arts Center’s site before signing up.
