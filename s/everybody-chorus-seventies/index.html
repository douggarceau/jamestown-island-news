The Everybody Chorus Is Rockin' the '70s. Anyone Can Join

No auditions, all ages, Saturday mornings at the Recreation Center, and the money for music books goes to charity.

If you've ever wanted to sing with a group but don't want the pressure, Saturday mornings at the Jamestown Recreation Center are for you. The Everybody Chorus meets from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. at 41 Conanicus Avenue, and this fall it's singing songs of the 1970s.

No auditions, no stress

That's the group's own pitch: no auditions, no stress, just a lot of fun, and everybody is welcome, any age and any experience. Singers can bring an instrument, too.

The Everybody Chorus is part of the Jamestown Community Chorus, a nonprofit, and is led by director B.J. Whitehouse. Each session has a theme. Past ones have included "The Weather," "Songs of the Road" and "The '60s Weren't That Long Ago."

It gives back

Singers pay $2 to $3 for a music book, and the chorus says all the money raised goes to charity. This spring, an Everybody Chorus event raised more than $600 for the Refugee Dream Center in Providence.

How to join

Just show up on a Saturday morning, or contact the chorus at 401-787-4487 or cantabile50@gmail.com. Details are at jamestownchorus.com.
