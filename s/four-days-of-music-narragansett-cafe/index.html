Four Days of Live Music at the Narragansett Cafe

Megan Chenot tonight, the King Vintage Band Friday, the Smokin' Toads' Halloween party Saturday and the Senders Sunday afternoon.

The Narragansett Cafe has live music every day from Thursday through Sunday this Halloween weekend.

Thursday, Oct. 29: Megan Chenot, 5 to 8 p.m.

Newport-based singer Megan Chenot plays an early-evening set. She covers Motown, pop and classic rock from every decade of the last century, and earlier in her career she toured the U.S. and Europe with the New England R&B and swing band Eight to the Bar.

Friday, Oct. 30: The King Vintage Band, 8:15 to 11:45 p.m.

The King Vintage Band takes the Friday-night slot.

Saturday, Oct. 31: The Smokin' Toads Halloween Party, 8:30 p.m. to midnight

The cafe's Halloween-night party has the Smokin' Toads on stage. The cafe hasn't posted a cover charge, costume contest or specials, so call ahead if those matter to you.

Sunday, Nov. 1: The Senders, afternoon

The Senders, a Rhode Island rock band since the early 1980s, close out the weekend. The band lists the show from 3 to 7 p.m.; the cafe lists 4 p.m. Read our profile, The Senders: Four Decades of Keeping Rhode Island Dancing.

The Narragansett Cafe is at 25 Narragansett Avenue. Call (401) 423-2150 to confirm times, admission and kitchen hours.
