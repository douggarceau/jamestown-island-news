Library Book Sale and Yarn Swap on Holiday Stroll Day

The Friends of the Jamestown Philomenian Library hold their Winter Book Sale at the library during the Holiday Stroll on Saturday, Dec. 5.

Book lovers have a reason to duck into the library on Holiday Stroll Saturday. The Friends of the Jamestown Philomenian Library are holding their Winter Book Sale at the library during the town's stroll on Saturday, Dec. 5, according to the library.

Gifts, wrapped

The sale is pitched as holiday shopping. The library says the tables will hold giftable books in like-new condition, and a gift-wrapping station will wrap your finds so they're ready to give. Light refreshments will be served through the day.

Bring yarn, take yarn

Knitters get a table of their own. A Yarn Swap lets you pick up yarn for your next project, or bring in a stash you'd like to part with.

All proceeds benefit the Jamestown Philomenian Library.

When and where

Saturday, Dec. 5, during the Village Holiday Stroll, at the Jamestown Philomenian Library, 26 North Road. The island calendar lists a 9 a.m. start; check the library's events page for the hours of the sale.
