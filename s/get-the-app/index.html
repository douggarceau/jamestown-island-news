Get the Jamestown Island News App

Put Jamestown Island News on your phone's home screen or your computer's desktop and open it with one tap, like any other app.

Jamestown Island News now works as an app. Once it's installed, it opens with one tap from your home screen or desktop, in its own window, with the latest news, the Jamestown calendar and the Holiday Guide.

There's nothing to download from an app store, and it takes up almost no space on your phone.

On an iPhone or iPad

Open Jamestown Island News in Safari. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow at the bottom of the screen, then tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On an Android phone

Open the site in Chrome and tap the green Get the App button at the top of the page. If Chrome doesn't show an install box, tap the three-dot menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

On a computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the green Get the App button at the top of the page, or click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. The app opens in its own window and can be pinned to your taskbar or dock.

Updates

The app updates itself. Each time you open it, you get the newest articles and listings.
