Last Call for Golf on Conanicus Avenue: Season Runs Through November

The Jamestown Golf Course's season runs April through the end of November, so there are still a few weeks to play the nine holes by the bay.

Golfers, the clock is ticking. The Jamestown Golf Course, the nine-hole course at 245 Conanicus Avenue, runs a seasonal schedule from April through the end of November, according to its Visit Rhode Island listing.

That leaves the next few weeks for a last round with a view of the bay before the course closes for the winter. The course hasn't posted an exact closing date, so call (401) 423-9930 before you head over, especially on a cold or windy day.

Off the course, the Conanicut Yacht Club at 40 Bay View Drive says it has two platform tennis courts for winter play.
