Halloween Night in Jamestown: What to Expect

The costume parade, the fire department's foam pit and the trick-or-treat streets around Green Lane. Here's how the island does it, and what to know for Saturday.

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year. The town hasn't posted 2026 times yet, so here is how the night has run in recent years. We'll update this story as soon as this year's details are out.

The parade

The Jamestown Women's Club sponsors a children's costume parade down Narragansett Avenue on Halloween. In 2024, families gathered at 4 p.m. behind Town Hall and stepped off at 4:30, marching to Veterans Memorial Square by East Ferry, led by a New Orleans-style swing band, the Catnip Junkies.

The foam pit

The Jamestown Fire Department fills a foam pit at the station around dusk, and in 2024 handed out candy, cider, popcorn and hot chocolate in front of its museum. Firefighters suggest trick-or-treating first, because kids come out of the foam wet and can get cold. The fire chief has described the foam as unscented, biodegradable, hypoallergenic and nontoxic.

Trick-or-treating

Most trick-or-treaters head to the neighborhood around Green Lane, Clinton Avenue and High Street, which has drawn several hundred children in recent years. Police close those streets to traffic for the evening. Residents there have collected candy donations at 20A Clinton Avenue to keep up with the crowd.

Getting around

Plan on Narragansett Avenue closing downtown for the parade in the late afternoon, and barriers around Green Lane and High Street into the evening. Park early or walk in.

Before you head out

Give every kid a flashlight or glow stick, and put something reflective on dark costumes.

Stay on sidewalks and cross at corners. Drivers, take it slow on the island after dark Saturday.

Check the candy at home before anyone digs in.

For the grown-ups

The Smokin' Toads play the Narragansett Cafe's Halloween Party from 8:30 p.m. to midnight. The rest of the weekend is in our Halloween Weekend on the Island guide.
