Halloween Party at the Conanicut Gift Shop & Arcade: Friday, 4 to 5:30 p.m.

20 Narragansett Ave., Jamestown: costumes, games, treats and a special guest at the shop's annual Halloween Party.

Get the costumes out a day early. The Conanicut Gift Shop & Arcade, 20 Narragansett Avenue in downtown Jamestown, hosts its annual Halloween Party on Friday, Oct. 30, from 4 to 5:30 p.m., in the shop's arcade.

"Join us Friday 4-5:30 at the Arcade," Marilyn Munger wrote in sending the event to Jamestown Island News.

What to expect

Come dressed in your favorite costume. The shop promises Halloween games, treats and fun for all ages, and says a special guest will make an appearance. Who it is, they're keeping a surprise.

Then keep the weekend going

When the party wraps up, the Narragansett Cafe is steps away at 25 Narragansett Avenue. King Vintage plays the cafe's Pre-Halloween Party Friday at 8 p.m., and on Sunday afternoon the Senders take the stage there from 3 to 7 p.m. (the cafe lists a 4 p.m. start; call (401) 423-2150 to check).

The details

Halloween Party at the Arcade, Conanicut Gift Shop & Arcade, 20 Narragansett Ave., Jamestown. Friday, Oct. 30, 4 to 5:30 p.m. Open to the public. The event page is on the shop's Facebook page.
