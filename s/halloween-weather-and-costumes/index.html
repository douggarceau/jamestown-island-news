Halloween in Jamestown: Will Your Costume Keep You Warm?

Saturday looks sunny and dry, but the evening drops into the upper 40s. Here's the hour-by-hour forecast, this year's top costumes and how warm they'll keep you.

Good news for trick-or-treaters: the National Weather Service expects a dry Halloween on the island, with sunshine Saturday and no rain in the forecast. The catch is the chill after sundown.

The forecast

Saturday is forecast to be sunny, with a high near 56 and a north wind of 5 to 13 mph. The sun sets at about 5:40 p.m. Saturday night turns partly cloudy, with a low around 42.

The wind should be light, but it can feel colder along the water. Near the shore, Mackerel Cove and East Ferry, expect it to feel a few degrees cooler than the numbers.

What everyone's wearing

In the National Retail Federation's 2026 Halloween survey, Spider-Man is again the top costume for children, with a generic superhero in second place. KPop Demon Hunters characters remain in the children's top 10. Among adults, Batman climbed to third.

Last-minute costumes

The nearest Spirit Halloween stores are at 5945 Post Road in North Kingstown and 1276 Bald Hill Road in Warwick. Hours run long in Halloween week but vary, so check the store's site before you drive.

Stay safe out there

Sunset comes early, so give every kid a flashlight or glow stick and something reflective, and drivers should take it slow on the island Saturday evening. Our Halloween Night in Jamestown guide has the parade, the foam pit and the trick-or-treat streets.
