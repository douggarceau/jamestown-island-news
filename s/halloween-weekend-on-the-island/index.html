Halloween Weekend on the Island

Live music three nights at the Narragansett Cafe, a sing-along, a film, a pumpkin vote at the library, and clocks that fall back Sunday.

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, and the island has a long weekend of it, starting Thursday.

Thursday, Oct. 29

Last day to vote in the Jamestown Philomenian Library's Pumpkin Decorating Contest. The decorated pumpkins are on display in the library, and anyone can vote once a day for a favorite. The pumpkins were donated by the Chase family at Hodgkiss Farm.

Little ones up to age 5 have Storytime at the library at 10:15 a.m., with stories and songs from Colleen and Erin.

At the Narragansett Cafe, Megan Chenot plays from 5 to 8 p.m.

The Jamestown Arts Center screens Once Upon a Time in Harlem with newportFILM. Doors open at 6 p.m. and the film starts at 7.

Friday, Oct. 30

Imprint and Playback, the Arts Center's exhibition of seven artists from the RISD Movement Lab Fellowship, is open from 11 a.m.

The Teen Center at the Recreation Center opens at 2:30 p.m.

The King Vintage Band plays the Narragansett Cafe from 8:15 to 11:45 p.m.

Saturday, Oct. 31

St. Matthew's Thrift Shop opens at 9 a.m. for shoppers and donations.

The Everybody Chorus meets at the Recreation Center at 9:30 a.m. for an hour of singing, no auditions. This fall's theme is songs of the seventies: the 1770s, 1870s and 1970s.

Imprint and Playback is open again at the Arts Center from 11 a.m.

Then the big one: the Narragansett Cafe's Halloween Party with The Smokin' Toads, 8:30 p.m. to midnight.

Sunday, Nov. 1

Daylight saving time ends. Set clocks back one hour at 2 a.m. That means an extra hour of sleep, and sunset about an hour earlier from here on.

Sunday services: Mass at St. Mark at 8 and 10:30 a.m., services at St. Matthew's at 8 and 10 a.m., and worship with children's programs at Central Baptist at 9:30 a.m.

The Senders close out the weekend with a 4 p.m. set at the Narragansett Cafe.

Times and covers can change, so check with the venue before you head out. Everything else happening on the island is in the Island Calendar.
