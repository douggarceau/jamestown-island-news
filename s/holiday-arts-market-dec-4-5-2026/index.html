Holiday Arts Market Dec. 4 and 5 at the Arts Center

Local artists sell one-of-a-kind work on Dec. 4 and 5, and an exhibition by Viktoria Lubchenko hangs through Dec. 12.

The Jamestown Arts Center's Holiday Arts Market returns for two days this December. It runs Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Arts Center, 18 Valley Street. The listing shows admission at no charge.

The market is a chance to shop directly from local artists. According to the listing, the two-day show features one-of-a-kind works, including ceramics, paintings, jewelry, textiles, woodcuts and prints. No registration requirement is mentioned for visitors. For questions, the listing gives a phone number of (401) 560-0979.

A show on the walls through Dec. 12

The Arts Center's September through December 2026 newsletter also lists an exhibition by Viktoria Lubchenko, titled "Portraits of Unspoken Truth." The show is on view from Nov. 12 through Dec. 12 at The Arts Center. The newsletter gives no admission or registration details for the exhibition, so visitors may want to call ahead.

Times come from each organizer's listing. Check before you go.
