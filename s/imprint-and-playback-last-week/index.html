Last Chance: 'Imprint and Playback' Closes Nov. 7 at the Arts Center

Seven artists from RISD's Movement Lab fill the Jamestown Arts Center with motion capture, sound and hand-tracked animation.

There's one more week to catch one of the more unusual shows to land on Valley Street. "Imprint and Playback" closes Saturday, Nov. 7 at the Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley Street.

What it is

The exhibition brings together seven artists who took part in the RISD Movement Lab Fellowship between 2023 and 2026: Azadeh Ahmadi, Patrick Buhr, Hunter Whitaker-Morrow, Kameron Neal, L.A. Samuelson, Maxime Cavajani and Melisa Achoko-Allela. It's curated by Africanus Okokon and L.A. Samuelson. The Arts Center says the work looks at how bodies, materials, ideas and memories move through time, landscape, storytelling and performance.

Three to look for

Achoko-Allela's "Songa" is an augmented-reality installation with sound, built from gestures in everyday African storytelling. Whitaker-Morrow's "Untitled (Realtime System)" takes apart Little House on the Prairie through a live, shifting system of sights and sounds. Buhr's "We move in circles to feel the center" uses hand tracking so visitors' movements link up on screen without touching.

Plan your visit

Gallery hours are 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., Wednesday through Saturday, or by appointment. The Arts Center lists no admission charge, and the building is fully wheelchair accessible. Questions: (401) 560-0979.
