An Evening of Kirtan at Island Heron Yoga on Dec. 5

Lisa Rubchinsky and Sarah Loomis lead call-and-response chanting Saturday, Dec. 5, 6 to 7:30 p.m., at 34 Narragansett Avenue.

Island Heron Yoga on Narragansett Avenue will host an evening of kirtan on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 6 to 7:30 p.m., led by Lisa Rubchinsky and Sarah Loomis.

Kirtan is a call-and-response style of chanting with roots in India's devotional traditions. One person sings a line and the group answers, so the evening works as music and as a kind of meditation at the same time. The studio describes it as a gathering to open the heart in community, and it notes that no experience is needed.

When and where

The session runs from 6 to 7:30 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 5, the same day as the island's Village Holiday Stroll and the evening tree lighting at East Ferry, so it can close out a full day in town. Island Heron Yoga is at 34 Narragansett Avenue; the studio's entrance is at the rear of the building.

Signing up

Places are reserved through the studio's online sign-up page, reached from its events listing. The listing does not give a price, so check the booking page before you go.
