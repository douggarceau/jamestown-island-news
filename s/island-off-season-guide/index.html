Off-Season on the Island: Ferry's Done, Here's What's Open

The Jamestown Newport Ferry and the town's museum and windmill closed for the season Oct. 12. Here's what's open and how fall cleanup works.

The summer crowds are gone, and so is the ferry. Here's a quick guide to getting around and getting things done on the island this fall.

The ferry is back in 2027

The Jamestown Newport Ferry's 2026 season ran from May 16 to Oct. 12, according to CruiseRI, which operates it. Its schedule page now reads "see you in 2027." Questions go to guestservices@cruiseri.com. Until spring, the way to Newport is over the Pell Bridge.

The transfer station

The town's transfer station at 1218 North Main Road is open Thursday through Monday, 8:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., and closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Fall cleanup is easy: the town accepts leaves, grass and flower debris from residents at no charge, and paper yard-waste bags are fine. Brush, trees and wood are not accepted.

Museum and windmill

The Jamestown Historical Society's museum on Narragansett Avenue and the Jamestown Windmill closed for the season on Oct. 12, according to the society's newsletter.

Holidays

Veterans Day (Wednesday, Nov. 11) and Thanksgiving (Thursday, Nov. 26) are state holidays. The town hasn't posted holiday hours for the transfer station or Town Hall, so call ahead or check jamestownri.gov before you load up the car.
