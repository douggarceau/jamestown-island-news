End of an Era: Island Rubbish Is Sold

The Teixeira family is selling the island's longtime trash hauler to Eastern Waste Services, and the town's contract goes with it.

For decades, the people picking up Jamestown's trash and recycling were neighbors. That is about to change. The Teixeira family, owners of Island Rubbish, intends to sell the business to Eastern Waste Services of North Kingstown, according to minutes of the Oct. 5 Town Council meeting.

The contract carries over

Island Rubbish holds the town's recycling and municipal trash contract, which runs from Nov. 1, 2024, through Oct. 31, 2028, and is worth about $1.19 million, with an option to extend another five years, the minutes say. Eastern Waste Services has given the town a statement saying it intends to honor the existing contract.

At Town Administrator Edward Mello's recommendation, the council approved reassigning the contract to Eastern Waste on its consent agenda, pending a final review by Town Solicitor Peter Ruggiero. The vote was 4-0.

A family thank-you

Mello told the council that Island Rubbish has served the community for decades as a local, family-owned company, and credited the crew's care at the curb with giving Jamestown one of the lowest recycling rejection rates in Rhode Island, according to the minutes.

Mike and Steve Teixeira attended the meeting. Council members thanked the family for their service to the island and wished them well in retirement.

> Decades at the curb, and one of the cleanest recycling records in the state.

For residents, nothing changes on paper: the same contract terms stay in place through 2028. Who shows up in the truck is the part that will be new.
