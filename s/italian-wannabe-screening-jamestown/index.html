Italian Wannabe Brings a Story of Food, Love and Starting Over to Jamestown

A one-night screening at the Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley Street, Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7 p.m.; doors at 6:30.

The Jamestown Arts Center will present a one-night screening of the independent documentary Italian Wannabe on Saturday, November 7, at 7 p.m., with doors opening at 6:30 p.m. at 18 Valley Street. The film follows chef and restaurateur Bill Disselhorst as he revisits the Italian community that helped shape his love of cooking and searches for a way forward after a devastating personal loss.

Disselhorst and his wife, Anne, built Fiore Market Café in South Pasadena, California, into a neighborhood institution. Their small restaurant became known for freshly baked bread, long lines and communal tables where customers gathered over carefully prepared food. The café's appeal extended beyond its menu: it offered the warmth and familiarity of a shared neighborhood gathering place.

The couple's connection to Italian food and hospitality had deep roots in their travels. A visit to Casperia, a hilltop village in Italy's Lazio region, helped inspire their vision of a café centered on simple cooking and community. Following Anne's death and the loss of the restaurant they had created together, Bill returned to Italy, revisiting the place that had helped define their shared ambitions. Rather than presenting an ordinary culinary travelogue, the documentary explores how a particular place, its people and the act of preparing food can become inseparable from memory, grief and the possibility of renewal.

The film has already attracted festival attention. According to the Jamestown Arts Center, Italian Wannabe sold out five screenings at the 2026 Palm Springs International Film Festival before beginning a series of screenings around the country. The Jamestown presentation brings that story to Rhode Island audiences for a single evening.

Italian Wannabe screens Saturday, November 7, at 7 p.m., with doors opening at 6:30 p.m. at the Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley Street. Ticket prices and any discounts are listed on the Jamestown Arts Center's event page.
