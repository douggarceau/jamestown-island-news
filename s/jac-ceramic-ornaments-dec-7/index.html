Make a Ceramic Ornament at the Arts Center on Dec. 7

A drop-in decorating evening runs from 6 to 9 p.m. on Monday, Dec. 7, for $15.

The Arts Center's holiday season starts with a drop-in evening for making something to hang on the tree. Ceramic Ornament Decorating runs from 6 to 9 p.m. on Monday, Dec. 7, at a cost of $15. Guests can come in at any point during the three hours, and the listing does not mention registration.

How the ornaments are finished

Pieces are fired over the weekend and are available for pickup the following Monday, according to the newsletter. Children under 12 need a parent's supervision during the evening.

Plan around the gallery show

The evening falls during the run of Viktoria Lubchenko's Portraits of Unspoken Truth, which hangs in the gallery from Nov. 12 through Dec. 12. A visit to the gallery and an ornament session could easily fit into one trip to the Arts Center.
