Thanksgiving and Friendsgiving Classes at the Arts Center

Three November cooking classes run from Monday, Nov. 9 through Nov. 23, with member prices listed alongside.

Cooking season arrives early at the Jamestown Arts Center, which lists three November classes in its fall newsletter. Each one carries a regular price and a lower price for members.

The classes

Thanksgiving Remix meets on November 9 and again on November 21, starting at 4:00 p.m. It costs $60, or $48 for members.

Friendsgiving Appetizers is offered on November 14 and 16, with start times of 3:00 and 4:00 p.m. It costs $60, or $48 for members.

Soup & Simple Bread is set for November 23 at 4:00 p.m. It costs $45, or $36 for members.

Signing up

The newsletter gives prices and dates but does not say how to register or which room each class uses. Check the Arts Center's website or call the office before booking.

Looking ahead

December brings more classes, including Pad Thai on December 2 and Merry Little Appetizers on December 7 and 12.
