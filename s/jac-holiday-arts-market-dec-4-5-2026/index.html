Holiday Arts Market Fills the Arts Center Dec. 4-5

Local artists sell one-of-a-kind work at the Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley St., on Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5.

The Jamestown Arts Center turns its galleries into a holiday shop the weekend of the Village Holiday Stroll. The annual Holiday Arts Market runs Friday, Dec. 4, and Saturday, Dec. 5, at 18 Valley St., according to the Arts Center's listing on Visit Rhode Island.

What you'll find

The market is built around local makers. The listing names ceramics, paintings, jewelry, textiles, woodcuts and prints among the work for sale, all of it one-of-a-kind pieces meant as holiday gifts or as something to keep for yourself.

It is a good stop for anyone trying to give island-made gifts this year, and the timing lines up with the rest of the island's busiest holiday weekend. On Saturday, the Holiday Stroll fills Narragansett Avenue, Santa and Mrs. Claus arrive by boat at East Ferry, and the town tree is lit at East Ferry Wharf that evening.

When and where

Visit Rhode Island lists hours of 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. on both days. In past years the Arts Center has opened the market with a Friday evening session, so call ahead to confirm Friday's hours. Admission is at no charge.

Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley St., Jamestown. Phone (401) 560-0979.
