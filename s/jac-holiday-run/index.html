Shop Local, Literally: JAC's Holiday Arts Market and December Workshops

The Jamestown Arts Center's two-day market opens with a pre-party on Dec. 4, followed by the Members' Show opening and an art-law session.

Holiday shopping on the island has a home base, and it's on Valley Street. The Jamestown Arts Center's Holiday Arts Market returns for two days in early December, according to the center's event page.

The market

It kicks off with a pre-party and early shopping on Friday, Dec. 4, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., then opens for a full day on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., the same day as the Village Holiday Stroll. There is no admission charge.

Expect one-of-a-kind work from local artists: ceramics, paintings, jewelry, textiles, woodcuts and prints. The market is juried by independent professionals, and artists applied by Oct. 1. Last year's market brought together more than 20 local artists and artisans. The full artist list for this year has not been published.

> Bring a canned good, leave with something handmade.

The center asks shoppers to bring canned goods or other nonperishable food to donate to the Martin Luther King Jr. Community Center in Newport; collection bins are in the lobby. The building is fully wheelchair accessible.

Registration is through the center's website, jamestownartcenter.org.

On the walls: the Members' Show

The galleries will be hung with the annual Members' Show, which opens Friday, Nov. 20, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.This year's prizes are handed out at the reception, and the Best in Show winner earns a solo exhibition in the Small Gallery in 2027. Both shows run through Jan. 9, 2027, so market shoppers can see them too.

And after: know your rights

On Sunday, Dec. 6, from 3 to 5 p.m., art law attorney Gale P. Elston walks artists through copyright, VARA, trademark and fair use, and how to register and protect work, drawing on her new book. There is no charge, but space is limited and registration is required.

The Arts Center is at 18 Valley Street; for information call 401-560-0979.
