Shop Local, Literally: JAC's Holiday Arts Market

The Jamestown Arts Center's two-day market, a members' show and an art-law session fill the run-up to December.

Holiday shopping on the island has a home base, and it's on Valley Street. The Jamestown Arts Center's Holiday Arts Market returns for two days in early December, according to the center's event page.

The market

It kicks off with a pre-party and early shopping on Friday, Dec. 4, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., then opens for a full day on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. There is no admission charge. Expect one-of-a-kind work from local artists: ceramics, paintings, jewelry, textiles, woodcuts and prints, per the listing. Vendor submissions are closed, and the full artist list has not been published yet.

The center asks shoppers to bring canned goods or other nonperishable food to donate to the Martin Luther King Jr. Community Center. The building is fully wheelchair accessible.

> Bring a canned good, leave with something handmade.

Before that: the Members' Show

Two weeks earlier, on Friday, Nov. 20, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., JAC opens its annual Members' Show along with a solo exhibition by Molly Kugler Dickinson, Of Longing and Loss, according to the center. Dickinson won Best in Show at last year's Members' Show. This year's prizes are handed out at the reception, and the Best in Show winner earns a solo exhibition in the Small Gallery in 2027. Both shows run through Jan. 9, 2027. The reception is open to all with no admission charge.

And after: know your rights

On Sunday, Dec. 6, from 3 to 5 p.m., art law attorney Gale P. Elston walks artists through copyright, VARA, trademark and fair use, and how to register and protect work, drawing on her new book, according to JAC. There is no charge, but space is limited and registration is required.
