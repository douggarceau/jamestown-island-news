New Portrait Show Opens at the Arts Center Nov. 12

Viktoria Lubchenko's Portraits of Unspoken Truth fills the Arts Center gallery from Nov. 12 through Dec. 12.

The Jamestown Arts Center has a new exhibition on the calendar for late autumn and all of December. Viktoria Lubchenko's show, Portraits of Unspoken Truth, is scheduled for November 12 through December 12, according to the Arts Center's September-to-December newsletter.

The newsletter lists the show among the gallery's exhibitions and gives the Arts Center gallery as its location. It does not state an admission charge or gallery hours for the show, so visitors should check the Arts Center's website before going.

Other dates in the same stretch

Several Arts Center events fall while the show is up. The Second Saturday family workshop, Martian Plant Life, is set for December 12, the last day of the exhibition. It is for students in grades 1 through 5, runs 10:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. or 1:00 to 2:30 p.m., and costs $15 per child, or $12 for members. Registration is online and must be completed at least 24 hours in advance.

The Ceramic Ornament Decorating evening on December 7 also takes place during the run.

What came before

The gallery's previous exhibition, Doug Pfliger's Fowl Play: Cause & Blame, opened October 8 and ran through November 7.

The newsletter gives only the title as a description of the work itself. For more about the work and the artist, the Arts Center's exhibition page is the best next stop.
