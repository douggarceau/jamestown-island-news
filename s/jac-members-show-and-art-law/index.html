Members' Show Opens, Plus a Crash Course in Art Law

The Jamestown Arts Center opens its annual members' exhibition Nov. 20 and hosts an artists' rights session Dec. 6.

The Jamestown Arts Center on Valley Street has two dates worth circling for island artists, and both come at no charge.

The Members' Show

JAC's annual Members' Show opens with a reception on Friday, Nov. 20, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., according to the center's event page.

That prize is the hook. Awards for Best in Show, second place, third place and honorable mentions are handed out during the reception, and JAC says this year's top winner earns a solo exhibition in its Small Gallery in 2027. Both shows run through Jan. 9, 2027, and the reception is open to all.

> This year's top member earns a 2027 solo show.

Know your rights

On Sunday, Dec. 6, from 3 to 5 p.m., art law attorney Gale P. Elston leads a professional development session built around her new book, Art Law and Artists' Rights for Creators, Authors, and Artists. JAC says she will cover copyright, fair use and VARA rights, along with trademark basics, registering artwork and protecting work from piracy, using real-world examples.

The session is offered at no charge, but space is limited and registration is required through JAC's website or by calling (401) 560-0979. The center is at 18 Valley Street.
