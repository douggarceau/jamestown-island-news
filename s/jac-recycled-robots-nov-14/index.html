Recycled Robots: A Second Saturday Workshop for Grades 1–5

Kids in grades 1 through 5 can join the Arts Center's Nov. 14 session, at $15 per child or $12 for members.

The Arts Center's Second Saturday series returns on November 14 with Recycled Robots, a workshop for students in grades 1 through 5. Families choose one of two sessions: 10:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., or 1:00 to 2:30 p.m.

Cost and sign-up

The workshop costs $15 per child, or $12 per child for Arts Center members. Registration is online and must be completed at least 24 hours in advance, according to the newsletter.

The series

Second Saturday takes place on the second Saturday of each month. The December session, Martian Plant Life, is set for December 12, at the same two times and at the same prices.

The newsletter describes the November workshop only by its title, so the Arts Center's own listing is the best place to check what children will make and what to bring.
