Holiday Arts Market Opens Dec. 4 at Jamestown Arts Center

Friday evening and all day Saturday at 18 Valley Street, with an art law talk the following Sunday.

The Jamestown Arts Center will host its Holiday Arts Market at 18 Valley Street, with a Friday evening session on Dec. 4 from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. and Saturday hours on Dec. 5 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., according to the Arts Center's own events page. There is no charge for admission.

A regional listing on Visit Rhode Island gives 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. for both days and describes the market as a two-day event. The Arts Center's page is the source for the Friday evening hours, so visitors should check it before heading out.

The market is presented by the Jamestown Arts Center. Neither listing names an RSVP or registration requirement for the market itself.

The Arts Center has also listed a talk on art law and artists' rights with attorney Gale P. Elston on Sunday, Dec. 6, from 3 to 5 p.m. The listing shows no charge. Times and any sign-up details for each event sit on its own page, so check there before you go.

Further ahead, the Arts Center lists a talk with Christopher T. Terry on Thursday, Jan. 21, 2027, from 6 to 7 p.m., with tickets listed as coming soon.

The Arts Center is at 18 Valley Street, Jamestown, RI 02835.
