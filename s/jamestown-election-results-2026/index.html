Jamestown Results: Voters Approve $3 Million Housing Bond

Question 6 passes with about 59 percent, and voters fill five Town Council seats in a race where the last seat was decided by 34 votes.

Jamestown voters approved borrowing up to $3 million for affordable housing on Tuesday and chose a new Town Council, according to unofficial results from the Rhode Island Board of Elections. Both of the town's polling places have reported.

The housing bond

Question 6 passed with 1,847 votes in favor and 1,288 against, or 58.92 percent to 41.08 percent. The measure authorizes the town to issue up to $3 million in bonds and notes for affordable housing, including buying property and developing housing on land the town already owns.

Town Council

Six candidates ran for five seats. The top five finishers were Marisa A. Quinn, Nicholas J. Insana, Richard J. Smith, Robert M. Bailey and Kirstin Renetta Reveron. Reveron finished 34 votes ahead of Mary G. Glackin, the closest margin on the local ballot.

School Committee and moderator

Christian Cowan, with 2,145 votes, and Sharon Rallis, with 2,099, were the only names on the ballot for two School Committee seats. Write-ins drew 39 votes. Bruce W. McIntyre was the only name on the ballot for town moderator and received 2,547 votes, with 49 write-ins.

Turnout

The state results page lists 3,294 ballots cast by Jamestown's 5,105 registered voters, a turnout of about 65 percent.

These results are unofficial until the Board of Elections certifies them. Updated totals will be posted on the board's results site.
