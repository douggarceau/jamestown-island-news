Costumes On: The Halloween Parade Marches Down Narragansett Saturday

Line up at St. Matthew's Church at 4 p.m. on Saturday, Oct. 31; the parade steps off at 4:30 and ends at East Ferry Memorial Square.

Dig out the capes, the cat ears and the homemade cardboard robots. Jamestown's annual Halloween costume parade is Saturday, Oct. 31, and the whole island is invited to march.

When and where

Meet in the St. Matthew's Church parking lot at 4 p.m. The parade begins at 4:30 and heads down Narragansett Avenue, finishing at East Ferry Memorial Square, right by the water.

A tradition in new hands

For many years the Jamestown Women's Club organized the parade, and the town is thanking them for it. This year Jamestown Parks and Recreation takes over, and the department says it's excited to keep the tradition alive.

Before you go

Come early to find the group, bring a layer under the costume, and plan on a short, festive walk through the village. Spectators are welcome along Narragansett Avenue.

For Parks and Recreation programs and registration, visit jamestown.perfectmind.com or the town's recreation page.
