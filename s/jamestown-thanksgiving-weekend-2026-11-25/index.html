Thanksgiving Weekend on the Island

Bands at the Ganny all weekend, galleries open Friday and Saturday, and Mrs. Claus sails from East Ferry on Sunday.

The bridge traffic thins out, the turkey's in the oven, and the island settles into a long weekend. One thing to know before Thursday: Rhode Island law keeps the big grocery and liquor stores closed on Thanksgiving, so stock up today, the Providence Journal has reported.

Music

The Narragansett Café keeps the bands coming all weekend: Eddy's Shoe on Friday at 8 p.m., Don't Think Twice on Saturday at 8:15 p.m., and the Earl Knightwood Band's R&B and soul on Sunday afternoon at 4 p.m., according to the café's listings and Bandsintown.

Art and a boat ride

Out of the Box Studio and Fuller Gallery are open for viewing Friday and Saturday from 10 a.m. On Sunday, Coastal Queen Cruises' Candy Cane Express sails from 1 East Ferry Wharf at 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. with Mrs. Claus aboard. Tickets start at $26, and every child gets a candy cane, per Visit Rhode Island.
