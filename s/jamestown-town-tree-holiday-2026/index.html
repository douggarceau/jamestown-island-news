Tree Lighting and Concert Dec. 5 Caps the Holiday Stroll

The island lights its Christmas tree at East Ferry Wharf from 5:30 to 7 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 5, after a full day of the Holiday Stroll and Santa's arrival by boat.

Saturday, Dec. 5, is the island's biggest holiday day of the year, and it ends the way it always does: with a crowd gathered at the water and the town tree coming to life.

The Tree Lighting and Concert runs from 5:30 to 7 p.m. at East Ferry Wharf, according to the Jamestown Chamber of Commerce. It is the last event of the Village Holiday Stroll, which fills Narragansett Avenue from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

What to expect

The Chamber describes the evening as the night Jamestown "lights up" for the season, with live music from local choral groups and bands. Boy Scout Troop 1's calendar lists the tree lighting and concert at 5:30 p.m., and the Scouts set out luminaries along the East Ferry seawall and up Narragansett Avenue, so the walk down to the wharf glows before the tree does.

There is no charge. The wharf sits right on the harbor, and a December evening by the water can be a good deal colder than the village a block up the hill, so hats, gloves and a warm coat are a good idea, especially for children who will be standing for the countdown.

Where to park and walk

Everything on Saturday is within a short walk of the Four Corners and East Ferry: the church fair at the top of Narragansett Avenue, the shops and restaurants along the avenue, the Arts Center on Valley Street and the wharf at the bottom of the hill. Beavertail is the one stop that needs a drive, about 10 minutes south to the state park.

If you can't make Saturday

The island's holiday calendar keeps going after Saturday: the Jamestown Community Chorus sings "A Contemporary Christmas" at the Recreation Center on Dec. 12 and 13, the lighthouse gift shop opens again Dec. 6, 12 and 13, and the Narragansett Café throws its annual Christmas party on Dec. 20.
