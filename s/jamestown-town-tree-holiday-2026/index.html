Tree Lighting and Concert Dec. 5 Caps the Holiday Stroll

The island lights its Christmas tree at 5:30 p.m. at East Ferry Wharf, the same day Santa arrives by boat.

Saturday, Dec. 5 is the island's biggest holiday day, and it ends with the lights coming on.

The lighting

The Tree Lighting runs 5:30 to 7 p.m. at East Ferry Wharf, according to the Jamestown Chamber of Commerce. Boy Scout Troop 1's calendar lists the tree lighting and concert at 5:30 p.m., with Scouts placing luminaries along the East Ferry seawall and Narragansett Avenue. Bring the family, sing along and watch the tree light up.

Make a day of it

Santa and Mrs. Claus arrive aboard the Coastal Queen at East Ferry Wharf at 1 p.m., and the Chamber of Commerce's Village Holiday Stroll runs from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. with craft fairs, open houses and holiday sales around the village. The tree lighting follows as the Stroll wraps up.
