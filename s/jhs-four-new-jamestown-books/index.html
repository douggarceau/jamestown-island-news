Four New Books Join the Island's Shelf

The Jamestown Historical Society tallies a burst of new island titles and digs into a collection reaching back to 1658.

Jamestown has been busy writing about itself. In an article posted Oct. 1, the Jamestown Historical Society reports that four new books about the island appeared in 2025 and 2026, along with a newly edited edition of a fifth.

The new arrivals

The society lists the four: Jamestown by photographer Onne van der Wal; Beyond Sight by Norm Marsh; Jamestown by John Tschirch and David Weisman; and A Peaceful Patch of Earth by Peter Fay and Valerie J. Southern. Each is entered in the JHS online catalog. The Fay and Southern book, on Black residents of Jamestown from 1850 to 1920, has also been the focus of a JHS talk and book signing.

Going back to 1658

The new titles join a collection the society says runs from 1658 to the present. The oldest is Roger Williams' An Answer to a Scandalous Paper; according to local historian Peter Fay, the Rhode Island Historical Society first published the full transcript as a book in 1945. JHS holds a 32-page reprint and transcription.

The earliest modern book devoted solely to Jamestown, per the society, is an 1899 volume on The First Movable Church, the Chapel of the Transfiguration. The church on wheels was conceived by St. Matthew's rector Charles E. Preston and designed by Jamestown architect Charles Bevins to serve farm families in winter and move to Conanicut Park in summer. Hauling it to its winter site in April 1890 took 20 yoke of oxen, and the society says it was so heavy it never reached Conanicut Park.

Shelf staples

Other island titles on the society's list include Walter Leon Watson's History of Jamestown, Rhode Island (1949); Sue Maden's postcard book Greetings from Jamestown (1988) and The Building Boom in Jamestown (2004); Terrence and Mary Stearns McGaughan's Dismissed with Prejudice (1994), about the fight against an oil refinery; and James C. Buttrick's Images of America: Jamestown (2003). The full list is searchable in the JHS online catalog.
