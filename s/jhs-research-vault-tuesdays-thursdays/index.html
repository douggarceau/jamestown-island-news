The Historical Society's Vault Opens Tuesdays and Thursdays

The Jamestown Historical Society's research vault welcomes visitors on weekday mornings, and a monthly foyer exhibit shows rarely seen objects.

The Jamestown Historical Society keeps a research vault inside Town Hall at 93 Narragansett Ave. The society's website lists morning hours on Tuesdays or Thursdays, from 9 a.m. to noon, and suggests confirming before a research visit.

Just off the Town Hall foyer, the society also mounts a monthly exhibit drawn from its collection. Each display features objects that are seldom shown to the public. The June exhibit included island maps and a footstool decorated with an etching of Jamestown made in 1950. Visitors can pick up a copy of the Jamestown walking tour while they are there.

Researchers with questions about the collection can write to the society's collections committee at research@jhsri.org. The society also maintains an online collection catalog and a series of articles, and it welcomes volunteers, members and newsletter sign-ups.

For anyone curious about the island's past, a Tuesday or Thursday morning is a simple way to start. Pair a stop at the vault with a look at the exhibit, and the trip takes less time than a cup of coffee.
