Baby Jam at the Library: Lap Songs for Babies Up to 24 Months

The Jamestown Philomenian Library's Tuesday lap-sit session brings stories, finger rhymes and small-instrument songs to the youngest island families.

The Jamestown Philomenian Library has a playtime for the youngest island visitors. Baby Jam is for babies up to 24 months old and meets on Tuesdays from 10:30 to 11:00 a.m. in the library's Meeting Room, according to the library's program page.

It is a lap-sit session. Children and the grown-ups who bring them take part together in stories, finger rhymes and interactive songs that use small instruments. The library says the half hour is built around socializing, sensory exploration and gross motor skills, so it is as much a chance for new parents and caregivers to meet other families as it is a sing-along.

Before you go

Registration is required. The library says sign-ups open one week before each session, and space is limited, so families should claim a spot early. When you register, count everyone who will attend, including siblings who come along but do not take part.

The library suggests arriving a few minutes early and bringing a blanket or something similar to sit on. The session is designed around lap time, so a soft spot on the floor makes it easier for everyone to settle in.

Check the library's online calendar for the next session dates, and watch for the registration link on each event page. The Baby Jam listing is one of the library's regular children's programs, and its schedule can change from week to week.
