Send a Thank-You by Postcard at the Library on Nov. 14

A Saturday morning workshop invites adults to decorate a postcard for someone in town they are grateful for.

On Saturday, Nov. 14, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., the Jamestown Philomenian Library hosts the JPL Happy Mail Project, a workshop for adults. Participants decorate a postcard addressed to a person, agency or business in Jamestown that they are grateful for.

The library supplies the materials, so participants need only bring a little gratitude and a few minutes to spare. The postcards are displayed before they are mailed, which gives the workshop a community feel beyond the table.

Good to know

Registration is required, and the listing shows 19 seats. The event is listed with a Sydney Wright Room location, but the same listing also describes it as online, so people planning to attend should confirm the format with the library before heading over.

The project is one of several library programs this month, which also include storytime sessions, LEGO club and a Tuesday movie series. Check the library's calendar for sign-up details.
