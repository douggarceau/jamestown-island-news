Knitting and Mysteries at the Library on Nov. 23

Needles Galore and the Monday Mysteries book group both meet at the Jamestown Philomenian Library on the fourth Monday of the month.

Monday, Nov. 23, brings a full afternoon of quiet, creative company to the Jamestown Philomenian Library. Two long-running groups share the day, one for hands at work and one for whodunits.

Needles Galore is a knitting group that meets on the second and fourth Mondays of the month, from 2 to 4 p.m. Beginners get instruction from the ground up, while more experienced knitters can bring their own projects and ask for help with tricky patterns. Newcomers and drop-ins are welcome, and the group is sponsored by the library's Friends organization. There is no charge to take part.

Monday Mysteries follows at 4 p.m. It is a book group that reads mysteries by authors from around the world, with each month spotlighting a different country. The group normally meets on the fourth Monday, which makes November's session fall on the 23rd. Anyone with questions about the group can reach the library by phone or email.

Both groups suit a grey November afternoon. Bring a project or a finished book, settle in at the library, and enjoy the company.
