Good to know

Storytime requires in-person registration. Book club members sign up and borrow the book at the Youth Desk. Tech Time slots are 30 minutes; book one at (401) 423-7280. LEGO Club materials are provided.

The library is at 26 North Road and is open Monday through Wednesday from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Thursday through Saturday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sundays.
