Family Movie at the Library: The Polar Express on Nov. 30

The Philomenian Library's Saturday family screening is The Polar Express, with breakfast treats and registration required.

The holiday season starts a little early at the Jamestown Philomenian Library. On Saturday, Nov. 30, families are invited to a morning screening of The Polar Express. The film begins at 11 a.m. in the library's Meeting Hall, and the library describes the event as aimed at children and their families.

The movie is the 2004 adaptation of Chris Van Allsburg's story. It follows a young boy who travels to the North Pole on Christmas Eve and learns lessons about friendship, courage and the Christmas spirit. The film is rated G and runs one hour and 40 minutes, so younger viewers can settle in for the whole trip.

Breakfast and registration

Breakfast treats are served with the screening. Registration is required, so families who plan to attend should sign up through the library ahead of time.

Who makes it happen

The event is sponsored by the Friends of the Jamestown Philomenian Library, a nonprofit group that finds and funds programs at the library. The Friends also promote their own upcoming events, which require registration, and they invite residents to join the group.

For more about the library's programs, its website is the place to start.
