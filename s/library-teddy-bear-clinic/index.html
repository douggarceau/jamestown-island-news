A Check-Up for Teddy at the Library

The Friends of the JPL line up a stuffed-animal clinic for Nov. 7, and a teen book club meets Nov. 4.

The Jamestown Philomenian Library on North Road has two youth programs coming up in early November, and the Friends of the JPL are behind one of them.

A check-up for teddy

Looking ahead, on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 10 to 11 a.m., the library hosts a Teddy Bear Clinic for children roughly 2 to 8 and their caregivers. Kids bring a favorite stuffed animal or doll for a playful check-up and get to handle stethoscopes, bandages and pretend shots, with the aim of making real doctor visits less daunting. The library says it is led by Stacey, a Certified Child Life Specialist, and sponsored by the Friends. Registration is required, and the listing shows 25 seats.

Older kids have a date too. The One More Chapter Book Club, recommended for ages 12 and up, takes on Stacey Lee's The Downstairs Girl in November; the library's homepage lists the meeting for Wednesday, Nov. 4, at 3:15 p.m. Sign up and borrow a copy at the Youth Desk.
