Tuesday Movies at the Library: Rocky and Top Gun

Anniversary screenings of Rocky on Nov. 10 and Top Gun on Nov. 17 run from 1:30 to 4 p.m. in the library's Meeting Hall.

The Jamestown Philomenian Library's Tuesday movie series brings an in-person screening to the Meeting Hall, and the next two titles are a pair of anniversary favorites. Rocky, marking its 50th anniversary, plays Tuesday, Nov. 10. Top Gun, marking its 40th, follows on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Both run from 1:30 to 4 p.m. Eastern time.

Rocky, Nov. 10

Sylvester Stallone stars as a struggling Philadelphia boxer who earns a rare chance to fight the world heavyweight champion. Talia Shire and Burt Young co-star, and John G. Avildsen directed. The film is rated PG and runs about two hours.

Top Gun, Nov. 17

Tony Scott directed this story of Maverick, a reckless fighter pilot who clashes with his fellow pilots, especially the composed Iceman, while training at an elite naval flight school. The listing gives the running time as one hour and 49 minutes, and the film is rated PG.

Planning a visit

Both screenings are listed for adults. The listings do not show a cost, so check the library's calendar before heading over. The series continues on Tuesdays through late January 2027, making it a steady afternoon option for the winter months.
