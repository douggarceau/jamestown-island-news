November at the Narragansett Cafe: A Band Almost Every Night

The Senders, 3 Wheelin, Deep End, the Strumdawgs, Jury Band and Don't Think Twice lead a packed month at 25 Narragansett Ave.

The island's favorite stage doesn't slow down for the off-season. The Narragansett Cafe, 25 Narragansett Avenue, has live music on most nights in November, plus trivia on Tuesdays and jazz on Wednesdays.

Mark Flynn is an acoustic performer the cafe says plays more than 250 songs from the '70s to now. 3 Wheelin is a cover band from Warwick. The Strumdawgs, an acoustic and electric rock band, have been playing since 2016.

The rest of the month

Listings for the second half of November show the Chris Mitchell Group (Nov. 13), Jury Band (Nov. 14 and 22), Ed Peabody & The Big Blue Thang (Nov. 15), an open mic night (Nov. 18), Megan Chenot (Nov. 19), the modern rock band The Complaints (Nov. 20), Signs of Life (Nov. 21), 21 Guns (the night before Thanksgiving, Nov. 25), Eddy's Shoe (Nov. 27), Don't Think Twice (Nov. 28) and the Earl Knightwood Band (Nov. 29). Weekend shows start around 8 p.m., and Sunday sets around 4.

The cafe is listed as closed on Thanksgiving Day. Those later dates aren't on the cafe's own calendar yet, so check narragansettcaferi.com or call (401) 423-2150 before you head out.
