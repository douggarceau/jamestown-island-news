Rock and Blues All Thanksgiving Week at the Ganny

The Narragansett Cafe, 25 Narragansett Ave., has live bands from Saturday, Nov. 21, through Sunday, Nov. 29, including the night before Thanksgiving.

The Narragansett Cafe has bands booked nearly every night of the holiday week, and the stage at 25 Narragansett Ave. is the island's main place to hear live rock and blues.

The big one: the night before Thanksgiving

Wednesday, Nov. 25, is the night everyone is home for the holiday, and the cafe has 21 Guns on the bill at 8:15 p.m. The Pawtucket cover band bills itself as high-energy, with a gritty sound and plenty of stage energy.

Sunday afternoons

The two Sunday shows start at 4 p.m., for anyone who would rather be home before dark. The Earl Knightwood Band closes out the holiday weekend on Nov. 29.
