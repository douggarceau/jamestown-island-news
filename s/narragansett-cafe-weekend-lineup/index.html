Rock Tonight, Funk Sunday: The Narragansett Café Weekend

Live bands two nights running at the Narragansett Cafe, then trivia on Tuesday.

If Fort Getty's bonfire isn't the end of your Saturday, the island's longtime music room has the late shift covered. The Narragansett Cafe has bands on back-to-back days this weekend, according to the cafe's own lineup.

Saturday: The Complaints

Tonight, Saturday, Oct. 24, at 8:15 p.m., the stage belongs to The Complaints, a modern rock band led by singer, songwriter and guitarist Dean Petrella, with Chris Cruz on bass and Anthony Marotti on drums, per the cafe's listing.

Sunday: Serendipity

The weekend winds down earlier on Sunday, Oct. 25. Serendipity, a Rhode Island cover band that plays rock and funk, starts at 4 p.m., according to the cafe. An afternoon set means you can dance and still be home for dinner.

> Two bands, two nights, one horseshoe bar.

Tuesday: brains, not amps

The cafe swaps guitars for questions on Tuesday, Oct. 27, with trivia at 7 p.m., per its calendar.

About the room

The cafe has operated since the 1940s, according to its site, serving pub fare and booking rock, blues and R&B acts on weekends around its horseshoe-shaped bar. The cafe says owners Mark and Esther aim to keep it a live music venue. It's at 25 Narragansett Ave., open 11 a.m. to 1 a.m.; call 401-423-2150. The lineup page does not list a cover charge, so ask at the door.
