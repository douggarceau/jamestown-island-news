Newport Classical's Christmas: Cantus, Messiah at Rosecliff and Frisson

Three December weekends of holiday music over the bridge: Cantus on Dec. 5, Handel's Messiah in the Rosecliff ballroom on Dec. 6 and the Frisson chamber orchestra on Dec. 12.

For Jamestowners who like their Christmas music live and classical, Newport Classical has three December programs, each about 15 minutes over the Newport Bridge and each played twice in one afternoon, at 1 and 3:30 p.m.

Christmas with Cantus, Saturday, Dec. 5

The vocal ensemble Cantus opens the month at Emmanuel Church with a Christmas program. It falls on the same day as Jamestown's Village Holiday Stroll and the Bowen's Wharf tree lighting in Newport, so it pairs well with an afternoon on the waterfront.

Messiah at the Mansion, Sunday, Dec. 6

Handel's Messiah moves into Rosecliff, the Gilded Age mansion on Bellevue Avenue, for two performances by a mixed ensemble. Rosecliff's ballroom is the same room where Newport Contemporary Ballet stages the second act of its Nutcracker each year.

> Handel's Messiah in a Gilded Age ballroom.

Classical Christmas with Frisson, Saturday, Dec. 12

Frisson, an 11-piece chamber orchestra, closes the series back at Emmanuel Church. The announced program mixes Baroque masterworks with seasonal favorites: the Sinfonia from Handel's Messiah, the Adagio from Bach's Concerto for violin and oboe, BWV 1060, the Winter movement of Vivaldi's The Four Seasons and Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 4. After that come a traditional Christmas sing-along, Frackenpohl's A Christmas Jazz Suite, Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker Suite and Frisson's own arrangement of "Carol of the Bells." The program is subject to change.

It is a broad afternoon: the Bach and Vivaldi for listeners who like their holidays with counterpoint, and the sing-along and jazz suite for everyone else.

Tickets and getting there

Tickets are sold through newportclassical.org. Emmanuel Church is at 42 Dearborn Street in downtown Newport, and Rosecliff is at 548 Bellevue Avenue. Allow extra time for parking on Dec. 5, when the Bowen's Wharf tree lighting draws crowds downtown.

More holiday music over the bridge

The same weeks bring The Irish Tenors' A Family Christmas to the Jane Pickens Theater on Thursday, Dec. 10, at 7:30 p.m. (from $79), and a one-night musical retelling of A Christmas Carol at The Breakers on Thursday, Dec. 17, at 7 p.m., sung by the ensemble Skylark and narrated by actress Christine Baranski. Both are in our Holiday Guide.
