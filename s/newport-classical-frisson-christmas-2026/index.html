Classical Christmas with Frisson, a Short Drive Over the Bridge

Newport Classical presents an 11-piece chamber orchestra on Saturday, Dec. 12, at 1 and 3:30 p.m., at Emmanuel Church, Newport.

Jamestowners looking for a seasonal concert across the bridge can find one in Newport on Saturday, December 12, when Newport Classical presents Classical Christmas with Frisson, an 11-piece chamber orchestra. There are two performances, at 1:00 p.m. and 3:30 p.m., at Emmanuel Church, 42 Dearborn Street, Newport, RI 02840.

The program

The announced program mixes Baroque masterworks with seasonal favorites. It lists the Sinfonia from Handel's Messiah, the Adagio from Bach's Concerto for violin and oboe, BWV 1060, and the Winter movement of Vivaldi's The Four Seasons, followed by Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 4. Later come a traditional Christmas sing-along, Frackenpohl's A Christmas Jazz Suite, Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker Suite and Frisson's own arrangement of "Carol of the Bells." The program is subject to change.

It is a pleasantly broad evening, or rather afternoon: the Bach and Vivaldi for listeners who like their holidays with counterpoint, and the sing-along and jazz suite for everyone else.

More from Newport Classical

The organization's Chamber Series continues earlier in the season at the Newport Classical Recital Hall, also on Dearborn Street, on Friday evenings at 7:30. On November 6, pianist Michael Leonardo Davidman, winner of the 2025 American Piano Awards, makes his Newport debut with works by Bach, Schubert, Franck and Liszt. On November 20, guitarist Raphael Feuillatre plays a program called "Latin Passions," with Spanish and Latin American music by Tarrega, Albeniz, Piazzolla and Villa-Lobos, among others. Single tickets for the series start at $45.

Tickets

Frisson tickets are $35 (Main) or $55 (Premier). The 1:00 p.m. performance can be booked at newportclassical.org/event/classical-christmas-1pm/, and the 3:30 p.m. performance through the ticket link on the Visit Rhode Island listing.

For an hour or so of live strings, it makes a gentle start to the holiday season.
