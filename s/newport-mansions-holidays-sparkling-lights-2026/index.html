Holidays at the Newport Mansions and Sparkling Lights at The Breakers Open Nov. 21

Three mansions dressed for Christmas through Jan. 3, a half-mile of lights at The Breakers on 22 evenings, and a one-night Christmas Carol with Christine Baranski.

Just over the bridge, the Preservation Society of Newport County opens its holiday season on Saturday, Nov. 21. Three mansions are dressed for Christmas through Jan. 3, and The Breakers adds its evening light show, Sparkling Lights, on 22 nights through Dec. 27.

The decorated mansions

The Breakers and Marble House are open daily during Holidays at the Newport Mansions, and Chateau-sur-Mer is open on weekends only. Each house is decorated for the season. The best-known piece is The Breakers' poinsettia tree, which returns again this year. The Preservation Society also searched this fall for donated evergreens 20 to 30 feet tall to stand in Washington Square and at Marble House.

In past years the mansions have closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day and closed early, at 2 p.m., on Christmas Eve.

Sparkling Lights at The Breakers

After dark, The Breakers grounds become a half-mile walk through hundreds of thousands of holiday lights, with music playing along the path. A Sparkling Lights ticket also includes a look inside the decorated mansion during the same visit.

Entry is timed, at 4, 4:30, 5, 5:30 and 6 p.m., and the house and grounds close at 8. Once inside you can stay as long as you like until closing. Tickets are for a set date and time, are sent electronically and are not refundable unless the event is canceled for hazardous weather. Regular Breakers tickets are not accepted after 3 p.m. on Sparkling Lights days. Prices and tickets are at newportmansions.org.

The Dec. 17 Christmas Carol is a one-night-only performance: the vocal ensemble Skylark sings the story while Christine Baranski narrates. Tickets went on sale in October.

Getting there from Jamestown

The Breakers is at 44 Ochre Point Avenue, off Bellevue Avenue, about 20 minutes from the village over the Newport Bridge. Marble House is at 596 Bellevue Avenue and Chateau-sur-Mer at 474 Bellevue Avenue. For an evening visit, the 4 or 4:30 entry lets you see the house in the last of the daylight and the lights as night falls.
