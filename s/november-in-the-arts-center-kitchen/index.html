November in the Arts Center Kitchen

Ramen from scratch, Friendsgiving starters and a holiday appetizer night: the Valley Street kitchen’s fall lineup.

The busiest room on Valley Street this fall might be the kitchen. The Jamestown Arts Center’s hands-on cooking classes run straight through the holidays, and the menu reads like a tour of everything islanders actually want to eat in cold weather.

Halloween first

It starts tonight, Saturday, October 24, at 4 p.m., with A Killer Dinner Party: a three-course Halloween dinner with spooky presentations and seasonal flavors, cooked by the people who will eat it. It runs $65, or $52 for members.

Takeout, made at home

Monday, November 2, is Homemade Ramen: broth, seasoned chicken, jammy eggs, noodles and fresh toppings, built from scratch ($60, or $48 for members). The class comes back Saturday, December 5. Pad Thai gets its own night on Wednesday, December 2.

The holiday stretch

Friendsgiving Appetizers (November 14 at 3 p.m., November 16 at 3 and 4 p.m.) covers baked brie, flatbread and a harvest salad. Soup & Simple Bread, tomato-basil soup with garden focaccia, is the bargain of the season at $45, or $36 for members, on November 23 and December 21.

December brings Merry Little Appetizers (Beef Wellington bites, cranberry-orange meatballs and a Caprese candy-cane board) on December 7 and 12,.

> Tomato-basil soup and focaccia, December 21. Bring an appetite.

Sign up through the Arts Center at 18 Valley Street, (401) 560-0979, or jamestownartcenter.org. Every date is on the Island Calendar.
