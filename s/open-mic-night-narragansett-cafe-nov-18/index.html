Open Mic Night at the Narragansett Cafe on Nov. 18

Musicians and listeners gather at the cafe on Wednesday evening, Nov. 18, from 6:30 to 10 p.m.

Narragansett Cafe is hosting an open mic night on Wednesday, Nov. 18, from 6:30 to 10 p.m. The cafe lists the evening under its live music category, so it sits alongside the band and solo nights on its schedule this month.

Open mic nights give local singers, songwriters and instrumentalists a relaxed room to try new material in front of an audience, and they are a good way to hear the island's players up close.

Planning a visit

The cafe is at 25 Narragansett Avenue. The event page does not describe the sign-up process, performance time limits or an age policy, so performers who want a slot should contact the cafe by phone at (401) 423-2150 or through the contact options on its website before the night.

Listeners do not need to sign up. Arriving during the evening is the simplest way to catch the performances.
