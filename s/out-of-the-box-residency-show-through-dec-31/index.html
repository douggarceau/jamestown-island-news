Out of the Box's Artists-in-Residence Show Runs to Dec. 31

More than 30 artists in residence share one collaborative show at the studio and gallery on Clinton Avenue, open from Aug. 8 through Dec. 31.

Out of the Box Studio & Gallery, at 11 Clinton Avenue in Jamestown, has a show that runs through the end of the year. The collaborative exhibition brings together work by more than 30 Artists in Residence and is listed on the gallery's exhibitions page for Aug. 8 to Dec. 31, 2026.

A studio for everyone

A 2024 Ocean State Media profile describes the studio as a space for artists with and without disabilities, where community and art are the focus. The article says several dozen artists are current members, and each has a drawer of their own for materials and work.

The reception has passed

The show's gallery reception was Thursday, Oct. 15, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. The exhibitions page does not list regular gallery hours, so check the studio's website before you go.

Earlier shows this year

The gallery's 2026 listings include The Art of a Curious Mind, which ran from March 12 to April 24, and Beyond the Classroom, a May show of work by young artists from Narragansett High School that ran from May 14 to 28.
