One Lane Only: Pell Bridge Squeeze Hits Monday

RITBA cuts the Pell to a single lane each way Oct. 26 as night work continues on the Jamestown Bridge.

Islanders who commute over the Newport Pell Bridge should budget extra time on Monday. The Rhode Island Turnpike and Bridge Authority has posted a travel advisory putting the span down to one lane in each direction for most of Oct. 26 and into the early hours of Oct. 27.

Westbound, toward Jamestown, traffic is held to a single lane from 7 a.m. Monday until 3 a.m. Tuesday, according to RITBA. Eastbound, toward Newport, the single-lane window runs from 9 a.m. Monday to 3 a.m. Tuesday. The advisory does not give a reason for the work.

The rest of the week

The squeeze doesn't end there. RITBA's weekly construction schedule for Oct. 25 to 31 lists alternating eastbound lane closures on the Pell on Tuesday, Oct. 27, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.; alternating westbound closures on Wednesday and Thursday, Oct. 28 and 29, from 7 a.m. to 3 p.m.; and alternating eastbound closures on Friday, Oct. 30, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Night work on the Jamestown side

On the Jamestown Verrazzano Bridge, joint replacement work continues on the overnight shift the authority set in late spring: Sunday through Thursday nights, 7 p.m. to 7 a.m. This week's schedule calls for overnight alternating eastbound lane closures from Oct. 25 through Oct. 29. Earlier this year RITBA warned that traffic plates in the work zone create a bump and asked drivers to slow down.

> Check the bridge schedule before you leave the driveway.

RITBA notes that its schedule is subject to change. The authority posts real-time updates on its travel alerts page and on X at @RIEZPASS. Tolling on the Pell is all-electronic, so there are no cash lanes to plan around.
