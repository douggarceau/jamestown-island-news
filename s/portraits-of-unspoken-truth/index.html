Portraits of Unspoken Truth

Viktoria Lubchenko’s portraits fill the Jamestown Arts Center gallery from November 12 to December 12.

Faces are coming to Valley Street. “Portraits of Unspoken Truth,” an exhibition of portraits by Viktoria Lubchenko, opens at the Jamestown Arts Center on Thursday, November 12, and stays up through Saturday, December 12.

Gallery hours are Wednesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and there is no admission charge. That makes it one of the easiest hours you can spend downtown this season: walk in on a Saturday morning, then stay for the kitchen classes in the afternoon.

Bring the kids

The Arts Center’s youngest artists get their own Saturdays. Step One, a guided introduction to art for ages 3 to 6, runs the first Saturday of each month (November 7 and December 5, at 10 a.m. or 1 p.m.; $10, or $8 for members, per child). Second Saturday for grades 1 to 5 builds Recycled Robots from discarded materials on November 14 and Martian Plant Life on December 12 ($15, or $12 for members; sign up at least 24 hours ahead).

And on Monday, December 7, from 6 to 9 p.m., anyone can drop in to glaze a ceramic ornament for $15. Pieces are fired over the weekend and ready the following Monday. Children under 12 need a parent along.

Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley Street, (401) 560-0979.
