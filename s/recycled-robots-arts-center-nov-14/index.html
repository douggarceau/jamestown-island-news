Recycled Robots Turn Castoffs Into Sculpture on Nov. 14

Students in grades 1 to 5 can build robot sculptures from discarded materials during a Second Saturday session at the Jamestown Arts Center.

On Saturday, Nov. 14, the Jamestown Arts Center's Second Saturday program turns to Recycled Robots, a hands-on workshop for students in grades 1 through 5. Young builders make robot sculptures out of discarded materials, so the afternoon is as much about seeing what an everyday object can become as it is about making something to take home.

Two sessions are offered that day: one from 10:30 a.m. to noon, and another from 1 to 2:30 p.m. Families can pick whichever time fits the afternoon. The fee is $15 per child, or $12 for Arts Center members. Sign-ups must be made at least 24 hours ahead of the session, so families who want a spot should register early in the week.

Part of a busy autumn at the Arts Center

The workshop sits alongside the Arts Center's other classes for young students. The fall and winter newsletter lists Step One art classes for ages 3 to 6 and Best Mug, a wheel-throwing class for grades 3 to 6 that meets on school days from Nov. 16 through Nov. 25.

Adults have plenty on the calendar too. The exhibit Portraits of Unspoken Truth, by Viktoria Lubchenko, opens Nov. 12 and runs through Dec. 12. A family that stops in for the robots can easily add a walk through the galleries before heading home.

The newsletter does not list what materials each child will need to bring, so parents with questions should contact the Arts Center before the session.
