Holiday Cruises from Jamestown: Mrs. Claus, Carolers and the Bay at Christmas

The Coastal Queen runs a Candy Cane Express with Mrs. Claus on Nov. 29 and a caroling cruise right after Santa docks on Dec. 5, with more carol sailings from Newport on Dec. 13 and 20.

The Coastal Queen spends most of the year carrying sightseers around Jamestown's shoreline and Newport Harbor. In the weeks before Christmas it turns into the island's holiday boat, with Mrs. Claus, carolers and Santa himself on board.

The Candy Cane Express, Sunday, Nov. 29

The season starts the Sunday after Thanksgiving with the Candy Cane Express, a family cruise with Mrs. Claus. There are two one-hour sailings from Jamestown, at 11 a.m. and 1 p.m., leaving from 1 East Ferry Wharf. When the boat's crew talked about the cruise on WJAR's Studio 10, they said Mrs. Claus reads books to the children on board.

> Mrs. Claus reads to the children on board.

Santa's ride, Saturday, Dec. 5

On Stroll day the Coastal Queen carries Santa and Mrs. Claus across the bay, docking at East Ferry at 1 p.m. sharp. Santa heads up the hill on a fire truck to the Conanicut Gift Shop, and the boat turns right around.

Caroling on the water

At 1:30 p.m. the Coastal Queen leaves Jamestown again for a 75-minute Holiday Cruise featuring the Very Merry Dickens Carolers, a group of carolers singing traditional Christmas carols. The boat's grand center bar is open, serving holiday drinks, beer, wine, mixed drinks and soft drinks.

If Dec. 5 is too busy, the carolers sail twice more from Newport, on Sundays, Dec. 13 and 20, at 1 p.m., leaving from 5 Bowen's Wharf Landing. Those Newport sailings are 75 minutes and cost $44 for adults, $39 for seniors and military, and $34 for children and youth. The Jamestown price for Dec. 5 has not been posted.

Booking

Seating on the holiday cruises is limited. Book through CruiseRI at cruiseri.com or call Coastal Queen Cruises at 401-423-9900. Dress for the weather, since December on the bay is cold.
