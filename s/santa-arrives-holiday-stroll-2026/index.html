Santa Arrives by Boat Dec. 5 for the Jamestown Holiday Stroll

The Coastal Queen docks at East Ferry at 1 p.m., then Santa and Mrs. Claus ride a fire truck to the Conanicut Gift Shop for photos.

Mark the calendar: Saturday, Dec. 5 is the day the holidays officially come to the island.

Santa comes by sea

Santa and Mrs. Claus arrive aboard the Coastal Queen, which the Jamestown Chamber of Commerce says will dock promptly at 1 p.m. at 1 East Ferry Wharf. From the wharf, the couple climbs aboard a fire truck and rides to the Conanicut Gift Shop at 20 Narragansett Avenue, where kids can visit and get their photos taken. The Chamber lists the visit from 1 to 2 p.m.

The Village Holiday Stroll

The same day, the Chamber's Jamestown Village Holiday Stroll runs from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. in the village, with church and school craft fairs and open houses at businesses around the commercial district, plus holiday sales, treats and festive music. The Chamber says more details are coming.

Sing along on the water

Right after Santa docks, the Coastal Queen heads back out at 1:30 p.m. for a 75-minute Holiday Cruise featuring the Very Merry Dickens Carolers, singing traditional carols. Book through CruiseRI at cruiseri.com or call (401) 423-9900; the Jamestown price hasn't been posted yet.

Before that: the Candy Cane Express

Little ones who can't wait until December can ride the Candy Cane Express Cruise with Mrs. Claus on Sunday, Nov. 29, with one-hour sailings from Jamestown at 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. Book through CruiseRI.

Then the tree lighting

The day ends with the Tree Lighting and Concert at 5:30 p.m. at the Jamestown Recreation Center.
