Santa and Mrs. Claus to Arrive by Sea for Jamestown's Village Holiday Stroll

The Coastal Queen docks at East Ferry Wharf at 1 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 5. A fire truck carries Santa and Mrs. Claus up to the Conanicut Gift Shop for photos from 1 to 2 p.m.

Santa doesn't drive onto Conanicut Island. He sails. On Saturday, Dec. 5, Santa and Mrs. Claus arrive aboard the Coastal Queen, which the Jamestown Chamber of Commerce says will dock promptly at 1 p.m. at 1 East Ferry Wharf.

It is the centerpiece of the Jamestown Village Holiday Stroll, the Chamber's yearly day of open houses, holiday sales and craft fairs that runs from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. along Narragansett Avenue.

From the boat to the fire truck

Once ashore, the couple climbs aboard a Jamestown fire truck for the short ride up the hill to the Conanicut Gift Shop at 20 Narragansett Avenue. The Chamber lists their visit there from 1 to 2 p.m., when children can tell Santa what they're hoping for and families can take photos.

Organizers have not published the fire truck's exact route. The simplest plan is to watch the boat come in at the wharf, then follow the crowd up Narragansett Avenue to the gift shop.

Tips for families

Get to East Ferry a few minutes before 1 p.m.; the boat is due right on the hour. The wharf is on open water, so bundle up the little ones. If the line at the gift shop is long, the Arts Center's Holiday Arts Market on Valley Street and the shops along the avenue are a short walk away.

The rest of the day

Santa's arrival is one stop in a full Saturday. The Central Baptist Church fair opens at 9 a.m., the Friends of the Library hold their winter book sale, the Arts Center market runs from 10 to 4, and the day ends with the Tree Lighting and Concert at East Ferry from 5:30 to 7 p.m.

Can't wait until December?

Mrs. Claus gets a head start. On Sunday, Nov. 29, she rides the Candy Cane Express, one-hour family cruises leaving Jamestown at 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. And right after Santa docks on Dec. 5, the Coastal Queen heads back out at 1:30 p.m. with the Very Merry Dickens Carolers. Both can be booked through Coastal Queen Cruises at 401-423-9900.

Final schedules and any weather changes come from the Chamber of Commerce, 401-423-3650.
