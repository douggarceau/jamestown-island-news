Santa and Mrs. Claus to Arrive by Sea for Jamestown's Village Holiday Stroll

The Coastal Queen docks at East Ferry Wharf at 1 p.m. on Saturday, Dec. 5, then a fire truck carries them to the Conanicut Gift Shop.

Santa and Mrs. Claus are scheduled to arrive in Jamestown on Saturday, December 5, 2026, at 1 p.m. aboard the Coastal Queen, docking at East Ferry Wharf before continuing into the village for a holiday appearance. The waterfront arrival is planned as a centerpiece of the annual Jamestown Village Holiday Stroll, a day of seasonal activities, shopping and community celebrations.

Following their arrival at East Ferry, Santa and Mrs. Claus are scheduled to travel by fire truck to the Conanicut Gift Shop at 20 Narragansett Avenue, where families can meet them and take photographs during an appearance scheduled from 1 to 2 p.m. The exact time they will step off the boat, the fire truck's departure time and its route through the village have not been specified.

The Village Holiday Stroll is scheduled from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., bringing residents and visitors into Jamestown's downtown shopping district for a day devoted to the holiday season. Participating businesses are expected to offer seasonal shopping and holiday activities, while community organizations and local artisans contribute to the festive atmosphere. A complete merchant-by-merchant program and performance schedule have not yet been confirmed.

The Jamestown Arts Center, at 18 Valley Street, is also expected to host a Holiday Arts Market from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., offering an opportunity to browse gifts and work by artists and craftspeople. Visitors should consult the Arts Center's event information for the confirmed vendor list and any associated admission or donation arrangements.

Families planning to welcome Santa and Mrs. Claus at the waterfront should arrive before the scheduled 1 p.m. boat arrival. The day's principal locations are East Ferry Wharf, the Conanicut Gift Shop on Narragansett Avenue and the Jamestown Arts Center on Valley Street. Final event details and schedule changes should be checked with the organizers.
