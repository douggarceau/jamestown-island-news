Senior Center Heads Home to West Street

Senior services move back into the renovated building in early November, and a Nov. 2 hearing could open the door to a north-end fire station.

The wait is nearly over for Jamestown's seniors. Senior services are expected to move back into the renovated center on West Street during the first week of November, Town Administrator Edward Mello told the Town Council on Oct. 5, according to the meeting minutes. A reopening ceremony set for Oct. 28 may shift slightly because of a scheduling conflict, he said.

Until the move, programs continue at the Jamestown Club House at 245 Conanicus Ave. Call Senior Services at (401) 423-9804 for the latest.

A fire station hearing on Nov. 2

The council also voted 3-0 to advertise a public hearing on Monday, Nov. 2, on zoning changes that would allow a fire or police station or substation, according to the minutes. Council President Nancy Beye recused herself from the vote.

Also on the agenda

The town is buying 73 Pemberton Ave. with a federal Community Development Block Grant and plans to offer it as an affordable home. The council moved the closing from Feb. 26, 2027, to July 1, 2027, because the current owners are still building their new house, the minutes say.

The council also accepted a $147,396 RIDOT road-paving grant that requires a town match of $294,792 by the end of 2029, and approved permits for the Halloween Parade on Narragansett Avenue on Oct. 31 and the Jamestown Shores Association Fall Fest at Heads Beach on Nov. 7.
