Sinatra by Candlelight at Central Baptist Church

A string trio plays Come Fly with Me and Strangers in the Night in two candlelit shows on Nov. 21.

Frank Sinatra's songbook is coming to Narragansett Avenue by candlelight. Lumos Experiences will present A Frank Sinatra Candlelight Experience at Central Baptist Church on Saturday, Nov. 21, with two one-hour performances at 6 p.m. and 8 p.m., according to the event listing on the Jamestown public calendar.

Strings, not a big band

There is no crooner and no brass section. The listing says a string trio will play the music: violinist Mina Lavcheva, violist Linda Numagami and cellist Zan Berry. The program includes Come Fly with Me, Strangers in the Night and the theme from New York, New York, arranged for three instruments in a chamber setting lit by candles.

The setting

Central Baptist Church, at 99 Narragansett Ave., traces its history on the island back to 1867. The Jamestown Historical Society marked the church's anniversary in 2017 with an exhibit of photographs, programs and other memorabilia. The Jamestown Community Chorus rehearses there, and the Newport String Quartet plays there this Sunday.

Tickets and details

Doors open at 5:15 p.m. for the early show and 7:30 p.m. for the late show, the listing says. The concert is ticketed, and seats are assigned by price tier, with spots inside each section filled in order of arrival. The organizer sets a minimum age of eight.

Prices and seating are on the Lumos Experiences website. Ticket questions go to the organizer; the church office can be reached at (401) 423-1651. As with any touring production, check with the organizer before you go in case times change.
