Halloween Night at the Narragansett Cafe: The Smokin' Toads

The Cafe's Halloween party is Saturday, Oct. 31, with the Smokin' Toads playing classic and newer rock from 8:30 p.m. at 25 Narragansett Ave.

The island's biggest Halloween night out is at the Narragansett Cafe. The Smokin' Toads headline the Cafe's Halloween party on Saturday, Oct. 31, starting at 8:30 p.m.

The band

The Smokin' Toads are a four-piece rock band with a set list that runs from the classics to newer rock, built for a dance floor.

Where

The Narragansett Cafe is at 25 Narragansett Ave. in Jamestown. Costumes are the obvious move.

And Sunday

The party keeps going Sunday, Nov. 1, when The Senders, Rhode Island dance-rock favorites since 1983, play the Cafe at 4 p.m. Details at narragansettcaferi.com.
