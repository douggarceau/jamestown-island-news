St. Matthew's Piano Concerts Have Played Since 2001

The Jamestown Community Piano Association has presented more than eighty concerts at Saint Matthew's Episcopal Church since its first in November 2001.

Saint Matthew's Episcopal Church hosts a series of piano concerts run by the Jamestown Community Piano Association, a group of local music lovers that has kept recitals going on the island for more than two decades.

How it started

The Association began in 1999 as a group of music lovers, many of them parishioners, who wanted Jamestown to have a fine piano for musical events. They raised $28,000 over two years and bought a seven-foot Schimmel grand piano.

The first concert took place in November 2001. According to the church's concert page, the Association has since presented more than eighty concerts.

What to expect

The series usually runs nine or ten concerts a year, most of them on Sunday afternoons at 2 p.m. The performers are professional and semi-professional musicians, and the church's page describes admission as no charge for the public.

Finding the next concert

The church's concert page does not list this fall's dates. It refers visitors to the parish calendar on the church's home page, so check there for the current season before you plan a Sunday afternoon.
