Story Alchemy Workshop at the Arts Center Nov. 7 and 8

Rachel Schaeffer Wolfe leads a two-morning workshop for adults on self-story, journaling and time outdoors.

Jamestown Arts Center is offering a two-day writing and reflection workshop this weekend. Story Alchemy, subtitled Becoming the Hero of Your Life, meets Saturday, Nov. 7, and Sunday, Nov. 8, from 10 a.m. to noon at the center on Valley Street.

The course is led by Rachel Schaeffer Wolfe, who holds a master's degree in the listing. The workshop aims to help adults move from self-criticism and people-pleasing toward self-compassion and a more positive life story. No previous experience is required.

What happens in the room

Participants work through four practices the organizers call elixirs: creative journaling, meditation, gentle movement and connection with nature. A central exercise, called Transformational Narration, asks people to look closely at the stories they tell about themselves and reshape the ones that hold them back.

Students are asked to bring a journal. Because the sessions are short and hands-on, the workshop suits people who have never taken a writing class as well as those who write regularly.

Cost and registration

Tuition is $110 for both sessions, or $99 for Arts Center members. Each order covers one person, so friends who attend together need separate registrations. The center also offers scholarships; the listing advises families who need financial help to apply for one rather than register and pay.

The workshop is one of several classes at the center this fall. Visitors can also see the current exhibition, Imprint and Playback, which is on view through Nov. 7.
