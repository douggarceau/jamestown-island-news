Mozart, Maconchy and a Quartet on Narragansett Ave

The Newport String Quartet brings its season-opening program to Central Baptist on Sunday afternoon.

Sunday afternoon on the island gets a serious soundtrack. The Newport String Quartet opens its signature Newport County Concert Series with a stop in Jamestown on Sunday, Oct. 25, at 3 p.m., at Central Baptist Community Church of Jamestown, 99 Narragansett Ave., according to the Newport String Project's concert listings.

The same program plays first in Middletown on Saturday, Oct. 24, at St. Columba's Parish Hall, so Sunday is the island's turn.

What's on the stand

The program covers four centuries in one afternoon. It opens with Franz Schubert's Quartettsatz, moves to the String Quartet No. 3 by British composer Elizabeth Maconchy, then to Jessie Montgomery's Source Code, and closes with Mozart's String Quintet in G minor, per the listing.

The Project describes Source Code elsewhere on its site as drawing on the choreography of Alvin Ailey, the poetry of Langston Hughes and Rita Dove, and the music of Ella Fitzgerald and Black spirituals. A quintet, by definition, needs a fifth player; the listing does not name who joins the quartet for the Mozart.

> Four composers, four centuries, one Sunday afternoon downtown.

Getting a seat

The listing points to a reserve-your-ticket form rather than a posted price, so check the Newport String Project site before you go, or call the organization at 401-330-0678.

Mark the winter date too

Jamestown gets the quartet back on Saturday, Feb. 6, 2027, at 4 p.m., this time at the Jamestown Arts Center on Valley Street. That program pairs Hildegard von Bingen and Schubert's "Death and the Maiden" quartet with music by Hemsi and Demirel, according to the same page.
