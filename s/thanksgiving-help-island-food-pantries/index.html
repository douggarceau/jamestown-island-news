Thanksgiving on the Island: How to Help Jamestown's Food Pantries

The Community Food Pantry and St. Mark's pantry are open through the holidays. Here's what they need and when to drop it off.

Jamestown has two food pantries, both run by volunteers, and both lean on neighbors through the holidays. Here's how they work, whether you need a hand or want to lend one.

Jamestown Community Food Pantry

The all-volunteer pantry is at 99 Narragansett Avenue. It's open the first Tuesday of the month from 10 a.m. to noon and other Tuesdays from 4 to 6 p.m., and on Fridays from 10 a.m. to noon except the first two Fridays of the month. Visits are by appointment; call (401) 560-4080.

Its most-needed food list: canned tuna or chicken, meals in a can, peanut butter, pasta and sauce, whole-grain low-sugar cereal, low-sodium vegetables, fruit cups, 100% juice, coffee, tea and granola bars. It also needs household basics: laundry and dish soap, bar soap, toilet paper, paper towels, deodorant, toothpaste, shampoo and conditioner, trash and sandwich bags, and diapers in all sizes.

Donations are taken on the first Tuesday of the month from 9 to 10 a.m. and other Tuesdays from 3 to 4 p.m.; the pantry asks you to call first. Volunteer applications are at jamestownpantry.org.

St. Mark Church food pantry

St. Mark's pantry is in the church basement at 60 Narragansett Avenue, open Mondays and Fridays from 9 to 11 a.m. Walk-ins are welcome and there's no need to pre-register. It's run by the parish's Outreach ministry, which in past years has also handed out Thanksgiving baskets provided by the Jamestown Rotary Club. To volunteer or ask about baskets, call the rectory at (401) 423-1421.

The easy way to help

Pick up a few things from the lists above on your next grocery run, and drop them off during donation hours. Every can stays on the island.
