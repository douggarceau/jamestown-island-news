The Island After the Season

Beavertail’s tower is dark until May. Here’s where Jamestown goes instead: the trail, the library, the Rec Center and a $3 lunch.

The summer crowd is gone, the Beavertail tower and museum are closed until late May, and Jamestown belongs to Jamestown again. This is the island’s own guide to the off-season.

Out on the shore

The Conanicut Island Sanctuary trail is a short loop with boardwalks and three overlooks above Marsh Meadows, off Conanicus Avenue. Parking at the trailhead off the highway ramp is tight. Potter’s Cove and Taylor’s Point, just east of the Newport Bridge toll booth, are open year-round for claming, fishing and picnics. Boat ramps are at East Ferry, Fort Getty and Fort Wetherill.

Indoors

The Jamestown Philomenian Library at 26 North Road is open Monday through Wednesday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Thursday through Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.; closed Sunday. The Recreation Center at 41 Conanicus Avenue runs boot camp, tai chi, morning stretch and pickleball, and is home base for the Community Theatre, Community Band and Community Chorus. The Teen Center next door is open weekdays 3 to 6 p.m., with a full-court gym, pool table, air hockey, foosball and video games.

Lunch for $3

The Senior Center at 6 West Street serves hot lunch weekdays from 11:15 a.m. to 12:15 p.m., with grab-and-go sandwiches from 11:30 to 12:30, for a suggested $3 donation; reserve at least 48 hours ahead at (401) 423-9804. There’s a Tuesday bridge group, a Bone Strength and Balance class and tech help through URI Cyber Seniors. St. Mark Food Pantry, 60 Narragansett Avenue, asks that you call ahead for hours: (401) 423-1421.

> The fire department trains on Thursday nights. Recruits can start at 15.

Join the department

The Jamestown Fire Department is recruiting. Fire volunteers can start in the Training Academy at 15 and become full members at 18, no experience required, with training Thursday nights. EMS volunteers need a Rhode Island EMT license, and EMT classes run each fall. Details at jamestownfd.com.

Coming in December: the Beavertail Lighthouse holiday open house, when the fire department’s Santa hangs a wreath on the tower, and the Holiday Stroll and tree lighting at East Ferry. Dates go on the Island Calendar the moment they’re set.
