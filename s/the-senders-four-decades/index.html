Sunday at the Narragansett Cafe: The Senders

Rhode Island's dance-rock favorites since 1983 play 25 Narragansett Ave., Jamestown, on Sunday, Nov. 1, 3 to 7 p.m. Band: thesenders.com · Cafe: narragansettcaferi.com

Few working bands in Rhode Island go back as far as the Senders, and few keep a calendar this full.

Four decades on stage

The band says it has been "rocking New England since 1983," and it marked 40 years together in 2024. Its sound, in the band's own words, is "a unique blend of danceable Classic Rock, R&B, and Modern Rock." Expect songs from the '80s and '90s right up to today. Singer and guitarist Dave Lema started the group as an acoustic act called ARA and gave it the Senders name in 1982.

This past summer the band played North Kingstown's Town Beach concert series, and it's a regular on Sunday afternoons at the Narragansett Cafe and the Ocean Mist in Wakefield.

See them Sunday in Jamestown

The Senders play the Narragansett Cafe, 25 Narragansett Avenue, Jamestown, on Sunday, Nov. 1. The band's schedule lists the show from 3 to 7 p.m., and the cafe's calendar lists a 4 p.m. start, so call the cafe at (401) 423-2150 to check. The band's full schedule is at thesenders.com, and the cafe's lineup is at narragansettcaferi.com.
