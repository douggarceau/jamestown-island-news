This Week on the Island: Jazz, Cooking Classes and a Movie Night

Live music four nights at the Narragansett Cafe, hands-on cooking at the Jamestown Arts Center and a screening to close the week, Nov. 1 to 8.

The first week of November is busy for a quiet-season island. Here's what's on.

For families

The Philomenian Library has a Tuesday movie, Christopher Robin, at 1:30 p.m. on Nov. 3, storytime for ages 0 to 5 on Wednesday mornings, and a Teddy Bear Clinic on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 10 a.m. The Everybody Chorus sings Songs of the Seventies Saturday mornings at 9:30 at the Recreation Center.
