This Weekend in Jamestown: Live Music, a Teddy Bear Clinic and Italian Wannabe

Three nights of music at the Narragansett Cafe, a Saturday film screening at the Arts Center, and the last day of Imprint and Playback, Nov. 6 to 8.

It's a full weekend on the island, from a stuffed-animal check-up at the library Saturday morning to a Sunday afternoon set at the Narragansett Cafe. Here's what's on, Friday through Sunday.

At the Jamestown Arts Center

Saturday night at 7, the Arts Center screens Italian Wannabe, the film about food, love and starting over. Tickets are $15.

Saturday is the last day to see the Imprint and Playback exhibition, open 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Story Alchemy, a two-day writing workshop with Rachel Schaeffer Wolfe, meets Saturday and Sunday at 10 a.m. ($110 for both days, $99 for members).

For families

The Philomenian Library holds a Teddy Bear Clinic with Healing Hearts on Saturday at 10 a.m.; children bring a stuffed animal for a check-up. On Friday, the Conanicut Island Sailing Foundation's Friday Explorations looks at microscopic life at Fort Getty at noon ($35 a session).

Around town

St. Matthew's Episcopal Church opens its thrift shop Saturday at 9 a.m. The Everybody Chorus sings Songs of the Seventies Saturday at 9:30 a.m. at the Recreation Center, and anyone can join. Out of the Box Studio and Fuller Art & Frame Gallery are open for viewing Friday and Saturday from 10 a.m., and Fuller is open Sunday from noon.
