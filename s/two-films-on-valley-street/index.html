Italian Wannabe Screens at the Jamestown Arts Center on Nov. 7

A film night at the Jamestown Arts Center on Nov. 7, about finding a fresh start.

Italian Wannabe

On Saturday, Nov. 7, doors open at 6:30 p.m. for "Italian Wannabe," with the film at 7, JAC says. It follows chef Bill Disselhorst, who with his wife, Anne, turned a tiny South Pasadena cafe known for fresh bread and shared tables into a neighborhood institution. After a tragedy cost him everything, he returned to the small Italian village where the couple first fell in love with food, looking for a reason to cook again.

The film sold out five screenings at the 2026 Palm Springs International Film Festival and is touring in pop-up screenings, per the center. General admission is $15 through the JAC events store.
