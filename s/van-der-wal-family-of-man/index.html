The Sailing Photographer Turns His Lens on People

Onne van der Wal shows the portraits behind his boat pictures at the Jamestown Arts Center on Nov. 4.

Onne van der Wal is known for pictures of boats. On Wednesday, Nov. 4, he's bringing the people instead. The Newport-based photographer presents "The Family of Man" at the Jamestown Arts Center from 6 to 7 p.m., according to the center's event page.

From the rail to the lens

Van der Wal was born in the Netherlands and raised in Cape Town, South Africa, according to JAC. He made his name sailing aboard the Dutch entry FLYER in the 1981-82 Whitbread Around the World Race, where his photographs from the voyage launched his photography career. He went on to become an award-winning nautical photographer and a longtime Canon Explorer of Light, and he teaches workshops and gives lectures, per the center.

A quieter side of the archive

This talk is something different. The slide show takes its title and inspiration from Edward Steichen's 1955 exhibition "The Family of Man" at the Museum of Modern Art, JAC says, and it showcases portraits van der Wal made on his travels: workers in shipyards in China, people on the streets of Cuba, villagers in Thailand.

> Shipyards, street corners and villages, seen by a sailor.

Through the photographs and the stories behind them, the presentation is built around the idea of shared humanity, according to the listing.

Details

Admission is pay what you can, with tickets through the JAC events store. The center is at 18 Valley St. and is fully wheelchair accessible. Call 401-560-0979 with questions.
