Wickford's Festival of Lights, Dec. 3 to 6, Just Across the Jamestown Bridge

Thousands of white lights, Santa arriving by boat at the Town Dock, horse-drawn hayrides and an Elf Parade fill the old village across the West Passage.

Jamestown isn't the only village where Santa arrives by sea. Across the Jamestown Verrazzano Bridge in North Kingstown, historic Wickford Village holds its annual Festival of Lights from Thursday, Dec. 3, through Sunday, Dec. 6.

For four days, thousands of small white lights outline the village's old buildings, and the shops along Main and Brown streets turn their windows into holiday displays, which are judged for awards.

All weekend there is music and dancing for shoppers, and the Boy Scouts serve hot cocoa. The Wickford Village Association has not posted exact times for each event; it points visitors to its "I Love Wickford Village" pages on Facebook and Instagram for times and last-minute changes.

Pairing it with the island's own celebration

Saturday, Dec. 5, is also Jamestown's Village Holiday Stroll, with Santa arriving at East Ferry at 1 p.m. and the tree lighting at 5:30. Families who want both can do Wickford on Thursday night, Friday evening or Sunday afternoon and keep Saturday for the island.

Getting there

Wickford is about 15 minutes from the village: cross the Jamestown Verrazzano Bridge, take Route 1 north into North Kingstown and follow the signs to Wickford Village. The festival is free to walk; the shops, restaurants and hayrides are the reason to bring a wallet.
