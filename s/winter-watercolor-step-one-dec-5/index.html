Winter Watercolor for Ages 3 to 6 on Dec. 5

Step One's holiday watercolor session has two time slots on Saturday, Dec. 5, at the Arts Center.

The Arts Center's Step One program has a seasonal painting class for its youngest artists. Winter Watercolor is for children ages 3 to 6 and takes place Saturday, Dec. 5.

Two time slots

There are two sessions, at 10 to 11 a.m. and at 1 to 2 p.m. The fee is $10 per child, or $8 for Arts Center members. Registration is online at JamestownArts.com.
