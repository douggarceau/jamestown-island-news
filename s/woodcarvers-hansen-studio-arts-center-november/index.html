Woodcarvers and Crafters Gather at the Hansen Studio

A weekly woodcarving group with no charge to join and a Saturday crafts club share the Jamestown Arts Center's Hansen Studio this month.

Hansen Studio, the Jamestown Arts Center's room for hands-on groups, has a steady rhythm of gatherings through November. The Woodcarvers Group meets on Thursday evenings at 6 p.m., on Nov. 12, Nov. 19 and Nov. 26. The group is open to carvers of every skill level. There is no charge to take part, and no reservations are needed.

The Arts Center describes the group as a place to whittle and to share woodworking advice, with skill levels of every kind welcome.

The Arts Center also notes that the group may not meet during holiday weeks. Anyone planning to attend the session on Nov. 26, the week of Thanksgiving, should check before going.

A Saturday morning for crafters

On Saturday, Nov. 21, from 9 a.m. to noon, the Arts & Crafts Club meets in the same studio. Crafters working in any medium spend the morning on their own projects in a social setting. The listing does not state a cost, so anyone interested should check with the Arts Center before attending.

The studio also hosts the Buffalo City Photo Club, which meets at 6:30 p.m. on Tuesday, Nov. 17. That group focuses mainly on monthly photography outings and is aimed chiefly at DSLR users. It carries no charge.

Together, the Thursday carvers, the Saturday crafters and the photographers show how Hansen Studio serves as a meeting place for people who make things with their hands. The room is a good reminder that the Arts Center's offerings extend well beyond its galleries and classrooms.
