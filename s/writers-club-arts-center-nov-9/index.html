Writers Club Meets at the Arts Center on Nov. 9

The Arts Center's Writers Club gathers Monday evening in the Hansen Studio, and the listing shows no charge.

Writers who want a regular place to share their work have one on Valley Street this month. The Jamestown Arts Center lists its Writers Club for Monday, Nov. 9, at 6:30 p.m. in the Hansen Studio.

The listing shows no charge for the session. The Arts Center's fall newsletter gives the date and room and points interested people to a contact through the Arts Center, which is the sensible first step for anyone who wants to know what each meeting covers before attending.
