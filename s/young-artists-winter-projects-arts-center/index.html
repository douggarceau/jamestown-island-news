Young Artists Get Winter Projects at the Arts Center

Step One watercolor on Dec. 5 and a Second Saturday sculpting class on Dec. 12 are open to young children.

The Arts Center’s Step One program is an early childhood class for ages 3 to 6, offered at 10:00 to 11:00 a.m. or 1:00 to 2:00 p.m. Its Winter Watercolor session on Dec. 5 paints winter trees inspired by folk art. The cost is $10 per child, or $8 for members. Families register online at JamestownArts.com.

A week later, the Second Saturday class for grades 1 through 5 meets on Dec. 12 for Martian Plant Life, a multi-media sculpting class. Sessions run 10:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. or 1:00 to 2:30 p.m. The cost is $15 per child, or $12 for members.

Signing up

Second Saturday sign-ups must be made at least 24 hours in advance, and seats are limited. Both programs are listed in the Arts Center’s fall newsletter, which describes Second Saturday as a class inspired by a yearly theme.
